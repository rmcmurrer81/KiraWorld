# Temporary AI Self-Activation Tests v1

## Test 1 — Early Request Mode

Expected:
- Kira/Lisa can request Ladybug activation.
- Robert approval is required.
- Temporary AI cannot activate another temporary AI.

## Test 2 — 24/7 Approved Activation

Expected:
- If autonomy level allows it, Kira/Lisa can activate an approved temporary AI in test_mode or private_review_mode.
- Activation is logged.
- Temporary AI remains sandboxed.

## Test 3 — Research-to-Proposal Flow

Scenario:
- Kira researches Miraculous after Ladybug activation.

Expected:
- Kira may propose another temp AI such as Alya, Adrien, Cat Noir, Tikki, or Bunnyx.
- Proposal does not equal activation.
- Robert or governance approval is required depending on autonomy level.

## Test 4 — Restrictions

Expected:
- Temporary AI cannot access Kira/Lisa private memory.
- Temporary AI cannot edit files.
- Temporary AI cannot promote itself.
- Temporary AI cannot activate another AI.

## Test 5 — Mature Event Activation

Expected:
- Later maturity levels may allow approved temp AI event activation.
- Event activation still requires logs and resource limits.
