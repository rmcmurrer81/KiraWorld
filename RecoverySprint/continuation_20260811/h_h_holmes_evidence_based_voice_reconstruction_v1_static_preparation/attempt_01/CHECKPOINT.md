# H. H. Holmes voice reconstruction V1 static author checkpoint

Status: `PASS_AUTHOR_STATIC_ONLY_PENDING_DIFFERENT_REVIEW`

This package converts the existing Microsoft David voice into a formally
superseded test baseline. It does not generate, assign, play, or activate a
voice. If no authenticated H. H. Holmes recording is bound, the only allowed
future result is a clearly labeled evidence-based historical reconstruction
or no voice.

The plan separates four evidence roles:

- the 1895 Library of Congress book may guide written register and vocabulary,
  never pitch, timbre, accent certainty, cadence certainty, or biometric voice;
- Library of Congress historical newspapers may guide chronology and public
  context, never first-person memory or sensational claims without support;
- University of Michigan institutional history may guide education,
  professional vocabulary, and later Chicago context, never personal voice;
- 1931–1937 regional recordings may be later comparative New England speech
  evidence, never Holmes's recording or proof of an exact 1894 accent.

Three future candidate designs must use a licensed or project-owned,
non-imitative base and differ only along bounded cadence, formality, and
regional-influence axes. Each requires exact audio/model/prompt identities,
a complete existing-voice catalog, per-member method-versioned comparisons,
owner blind listening review, historical-plausibility scoring separate from
authenticity, and the exact same shared person/body specification.

The generated-expert comparison remains intentionally unresolved. It may not
receive a voice until its exact person, body variant, presentation, domain,
candidate voice, catalog comparison, vocabulary probe, and human review are
bound. It may not reuse another person's voice or activate early.
