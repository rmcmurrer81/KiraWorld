# Mandatory post-freeze voice input for V21 or later

This input arrived after the V20 author seal. It is therefore **not** a reason
to reject V20, but it is mandatory for the next author package and any future
Temporary Creator / Avatar Builder / voice-generator executor.

1. When no recording of a historical person's voice survives, do not silently
   use a generic system voice. Produce an evidence-based designed
   reconstruction from the person's time period, places of origin and
   residence, likely language/dialect evidence, age and other source-supported
   characteristics. Record every source, inference, uncertainty, and excluded
   stereotype. Label the result `EVIDENCE_BASED_DESIGNED_RECONSTRUCTION`, never
   `SOURCE_RECORDING_BACKED_MATCH` or historically authentic.
2. Use the already-created H. H. Holmes variant as a regression case. The test
   must show that the system does not relabel a Windows male fallback as his
   authentic voice and that an evidence-based reconstruction is replaceable as
   better evidence becomes available.
3. For generated experts, the Creator chooses a male or female body/person
   specification and the Avatar Builder supplies the exact body-spec digest.
   The voice generator consumes that same sealed person-spec digest, creates a
   compatible adult voice, and proves it is perceptually distinct from all
   active people rather than reusing one generic expert voice.
4. Voice/body compatibility is coordination, not stereotyping: the selected
   voice must honor the person's explicit identity and body specification while
   allowing natural variation in pitch, resonance, accent, cadence, and age.
   The generated expert can later choose a different voice through an
   append-only, consent-bound replacement without changing identity or body
   history.
5. A V21 test battery must include source-ledger completeness, inference versus
   fact separation, uniqueness/collision checks, body/person-spec digest
   equality, provenance labeling, uncertainty, and later replacement.

No historical voice, expert voice, model, GPU, audio, or playback operation was
performed while recording this input.
