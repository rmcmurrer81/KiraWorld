# Mars facts and fictional habitat policy

This is a factual/design audit of experimental World 073, recorded separately from its source backup. Installed World 056 is unchanged. It does not assert NASA or ESA certification, working life-support systems, scientific simulation accuracy, headset readiness or owner appearance approval.

Use official primary sources for statements presented as real Mars science: NASA, NASA/JPL, ESA, instrument teams and relevant government or research-agency archives. Preserve the exact quantity and units, the source URL, definition and date checked. For observations also retain the observation date, location and instrument; publication or webpage-update dates are separate. A historic measurement must not be labelled current weather. A planet mean must not be turned into a measurement at the fictional base.

Keep four categories distinct in visible displays and machine-readable metadata:

1. Planet references with their definitions and appropriate approximation.
2. Dated observations tied to the actual mission, instrument and location.
3. Original base scenarios, tasks and comfort targets, visibly labelled fictional or illustrative.
4. Inferences or future engineering concepts, identified as proposals with implementation and validation limits.

Official habitat research supports design questions, not this prototype's particular settings, crop outcomes, shielding, pressure seals or thermal performance. Geometric hatch closure is not pressure equalization, functioning latches or verified airtightness. Original terrain, sky and floor plans are not surveyed Mars data. Artistic references can guide appearance but cannot establish scientific facts.

`planet-weather-review.md` and `claim-ledger.json` record the planet/weather checks and existing exported-artifact identities. `greenhouse-airlock-review.md` records a separate concept audit. The unfinished greenhouse claim ledger, private source fixtures, raw local receipts, owner state and GLB/media files are excluded. Public hash metadata identifies the inspected artifacts; it does not reproduce those private artifacts or make all local checks runnable from this folder alone.

Future consumers must preserve provenance and scenario labels. A static exported door-status texture is a starting snapshot: if doors become interactive, update it from actual local controller state or identify it as frozen. Never invent a pressure sensor or turn illustrative crop readings into live telemetry. New claims or revised reference values require another source check and an explicit versioned update; do not silently rewrite historical evidence.
