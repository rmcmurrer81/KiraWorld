frames, units, action/service names, joint mapping, timing limits, or simulator
commands. A simulator or robot demonstration requires an authoritative Hanson
target and the applicable safety/operating constraints.

The desired next evidence is:

2. run one accepted high-level sequence;
3. run one intentionally rejected sequence;
4. collect requested, accepted, started, completed/rejected/interrupted events;
5. validate and return the complete evidence chain to the variant's continuity
   record; and
6. disconnect safely on timeout, heartbeat loss, authorization loss, or policy
   failure.

## Explicit non-claims

This handoff does not claim:

- compatibility with an unreleased or unspecified Hanson interface;
- physical-robot safety certification or permission to deploy;
- a live robot connection, ROS 2 discovery, or simulator execution;
- literal consciousness, personhood, mental-health assessment, or biological
  emotion;
- literal transfer of a person or consciousness into a body;
- that a generated factual claim is true without evidence and review;
- that a private-reflection note reveals hidden chain-of-thought;
- that the two private voice assets may be publicly or onward redistributed;
  their use is limited by their recorded named-reviewer authorizations; or
- that the portable variants are production-ready or complete Mind V21
  implementations.

Mind V21's static autonomy rule must not be read as a disclosure bypass. It
prevents an owner or operator from governing Kira's viewpoint, speech, or
memory choices; it does not authorize this runtime to expose third-party
private data, credentials, biometric material, legally restricted records, or
anything outside a reviewed export boundary.
