# Louvre Under-Pyramid Lobby Blueprint Draft

## Purpose

Blueprint-first reconstruction note for the Louvre Pyramid interior/lobby pass. This is a working plan for the notebook-world prototype, not an official museum plan.

Important correction on 2026-06-29: the guessed under-pyramid blockout was disabled from live rendering because it looked fake and let the user end up inside/through the Pyramid glass. Interior construction is paused until the exterior Pyramid/entrance section is rebuilt from the main Louvre world-generator blueprint.

## Reference Inputs

Robert supplied multiple under-pyramid photos showing:

- Glass pyramid lattice overhead with the old Louvre palace visible through the glass.
- A large polished stone lower lobby floor.
- A concrete upper mezzanine/deck below the pyramid.
- Square/rectangular concrete columns supporting the deck.
- A coffered underside pattern on the concrete deck.
- Broad stairs, escalators, and a curved/spiral stair or ramp form.
- Queue stanchions, signage, visitor groups, and wall openings around the perimeter.

## Approximate Spatial Plan

Coordinate convention for the current prototype:

- Courtyard level: `y = 0`.
- Under-pyramid lobby floor: estimated at `y = -10`.
- Upper concrete deck/mezzanine under glass: estimated around `y = -2.4`.
- Main glass pyramid footprint remains centered at world origin, matching the existing 35 m square / 21.6 m high seed.

Main masses:

- Lower lobby: broad rectangular polished floor, about 88 m x 70 m in the current draft.
- Central upper deck: concrete slab under the pyramid, centered below glass.
- Central support pier: square concrete pier near the middle of the deck.
- Perimeter columns: four larger rectangular concrete columns as first-pass anchors.
- Back/perimeter walls: warm stone wall masses for ticket/security/shop openings.

## Vertical Section

From top to bottom:

- Glass pyramid lattice and metal mullions.
- Concrete ring/deck below glass.
- Coffered underside panels under the deck.
- Stairs/escalators/curved stair connecting deck and lower lobby.
- Lower polished lobby floor with visitors and queue lines.

## Movement Plan

Current controls:

- `M` no longer teleports into the guessed lobby.
- Courtyard walking remains at eye height `y = 1.68`.
- The Pyramid glass footprint now blocks walking until a researched entrance route exists.
- Interior walking should not be re-enabled until a sourced entrance/lobby blueprint is reviewed.
