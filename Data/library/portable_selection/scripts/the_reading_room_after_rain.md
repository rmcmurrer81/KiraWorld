# The Reading Room After Rain

Project-original short script for the portable non-adult reading collection.

This is fiction. It does not record a lived event or personal memory.

## Scene

Interior, a small reading room just after sunset. Rain beads on the windows. A table lamp is off. Two readers stand beside three shelves marked HISTORY, NATURE, and SURPRISE ME.

**READER ONE:** The rain stopped, but the windows are still telling the story.

**READER TWO:** Then let the room stay quiet for one more page.

**READER ONE:** Which shelf? History, nature, or the one marked "surprise me"?

**READER TWO:** History first. Surprise needs a little trust.

**READER ONE:** One chapter, then tea.

**READER TWO:** One chapter, and permission to stop at half a chapter.

Reader One takes an old city history from the shelf. Reader Two switches on the table lamp.

**READER ONE:** If the book makes a claim, we keep the page number.

**READER TWO:** If it makes us curious, we keep the question.

**READER ONE:** And if it makes us sleepy?

**READER TWO:** We keep the bookmark.

They sit. The room becomes quiet except for the turning of one page.

End.
