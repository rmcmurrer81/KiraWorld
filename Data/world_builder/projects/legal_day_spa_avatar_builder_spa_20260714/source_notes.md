# Legal Day Spa Source Notes

Status: source notes prepared for World Builder. No 3D placement approved.

## Sources Checked

- New Jersey Board of Massage and Bodywork Therapy: `https://www.njconsumeraffairs.gov/mbt`
  - Use as legal context that massage/bodywork is a licensed profession, not a casual unregulated service.
- New Jersey Massage and Bodywork Therapist Employer Registration: `https://www.njconsumeraffairs.gov/mbt/Pages/business.aspx`
  - Use for legitimate employer/business cues such as registration and liability-insurance context.
- ADA Title III businesses open to the public: `https://www.ada.gov/topics/title-iii/`
  - Use for public-accommodation accessibility expectations.
- ADA Small Business Primer: `https://www.ada.gov/resources/title-iii-primer/`
  - Use for accessible entrance/route/restroom/service-policy thinking.
- Dermascope spa layout article: `https://www.dermascope.com/spectacular-spa-structure-how-to-maximize-space-layout-and-design/`
  - Use only as layout inspiration: reception/waiting, consultation, treatment rooms, storage/break area, and smooth traffic flow.

## Derived Builder Rules

- The spa must be public, clean, licensed-looking, and accessible.
- The reception desk should be visible from entry.
- Treatment and Avatar Builder spaces should be private but not hidden or suspicious.
- Corridors must be clear and wide enough for avatar navigation.
- The Avatar Builder room is not a normal treatment room; it needs a talk button, preview display, consent/approval panel, and body-owner approval gate.
- The spa is not imported into Home World until Robert approves the standalone proof.
