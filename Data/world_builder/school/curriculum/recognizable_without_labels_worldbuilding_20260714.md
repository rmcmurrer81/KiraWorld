# Recognizable Without Labels Worldbuilding

Created: 2026-07-14

Robert review basis:

## Core Rule

A world or building must be recognizable before labels are shown. Labels are allowed for review and debugging, but they cannot carry the visual identity of the place.

Automatic F:
- A room or object can only be identified by text.
- Furniture is only boxes.
- Exterior is only a plain wall/roof/door placeholder.
- Door frames, thresholds, and handles are missing or misaligned.
- Routes require teleporting, clipping, or walking through blocked doorways.

## Required Spa Proofs

The next legal day spa preview must include:
- Exterior facade with real entrance cues, windows/glass, sign placement, roof/fascia/trim, lighting, plants, and scale.
- Reception/waiting room with recognizable counter, sofa/chairs, product/service cues, license wall, and public route.
- Avatar Builder studio with a reachable talk button, mirror/preview screen, chair/standing area, and consent/approval station.
- Treatment room, relaxation lounge, accessible restroom, staff/laundry/storage, and mechanical area.
- Real doors with frame, handle, open/close state, threshold, and inside/outside route targets.
- Labeled and unlabeled contact sheets.
- Collision/nav report proving an avatar can enter, move through, use the Avatar Builder station, and exit.
