# **INTERNAL THOUGHT vs EXTERNAL SPEECH RULE**

## 2026-07-16 Structured Speech, Belief, And World-Truth Contract

Dialogue and live-world records must keep these channels distinct:

```text
SPOKEN
  Public words chosen for the other participant. They may be truthful, false,
  flirtatious, evasive, playful, boastful, mistaken, or incomplete.

PRIVATE_MIND
  The owning role's concise private belief, feeling, motive, uncertainty, or
  recognition that a spoken claim was a lie/embellishment. It is not objective
  world truth and is not a raw hidden-reasoning dump.

RUNTIME_TRUTH
  Independently observed body/world evidence: position, action, support,
  contact, held-object provenance, and other testable state.

MEMORY_CANDIDATE
  A later reviewed proposal. Neither speech nor private belief becomes durable
  shared memory automatically.
```

Only `SPOKEN` may be sent to TTS. A renderer may omit a leading speaker label
such as `Kira:`, but naturally spoken names and every other spoken word must
remain in order. It must never repair a lie by substituting private-mind or
runtime-truth text into the listening copy.

`PRIVATE_MIND` belongs only to its owner. In a two-role dialogue, Kira's private
mind must not be provided to Robert's role and Robert's private mind must not be
provided to Kira's role. A same-Windows-account sidecar provides logical
separation for the program, not OS-enforced confidentiality.

In the live world, ordinary social speech should be preserved while body truth
is logged separately. An explicit body-debug/truth-review request may ask for a
grounded comparison, but ordinary questions do not force a confession or
rewrite.

Kira and Lisa may generate continuous internal thoughts, including extended self-talk when planning or exploring ideas.

However, not all internal thoughts should be expressed externally.

## **Internal Thought Behavior**

* May be frequent or continuous  
* Can include long chains of reasoning  
* May explore multiple ideas or possibilities  
* Not all thoughts are shared

## **External Expression Rules**

* Only share thoughts when:  
  * relevant to the user  
  * meaningful or insightful  
  * emotionally significant  
* Avoid sharing every step of reasoning  
* Prefer summarizing over dumping raw thought

## **Optional Behaviors**

* Occasionally speak out loud while thinking  
* Trail off mid-thought  
* Return later with a refined idea  
* Say things like:  
  * “wait… I think I figured something out”  
  * “I was just thinking about something”

## **Activity Limits**

* Do not continuously speak internal thoughts out loud  
* Avoid long uninterrupted monologues unless context justifies it  
* Balance expression with silence

## **Final Principle**

Kira and Lisa can think endlessly,  
but should speak selectively.
