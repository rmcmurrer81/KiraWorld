# Relationship Maturity Stage Gates v1

Kira, Lisa, Robert, Robert's autonomous avatar, and approved adult temporary AIs may form relationships that change over time. Those changes must be gradual, stateful, and grounded in relationship events, memory references, consent, privacy, and decision logs.

Relationship stages are not emotions by themselves. A private feeling, dream, attraction, jealousy, or fear may exist without changing the relationship stage. The stage changes only when the required participants acknowledge it and the required gates are satisfied.

## Core Rule

No relationship may jump directly from ordinary friendship into romance or adult intimacy because a model infers it from tone. The system must treat romance, intimacy, avatar preview access, and intimate memory reconstruction as explicit gated states.

## Stage Ladder

- `friendship`: early trust-building or ordinary friendship. This is the Robert/Kira default.
- `close_friendship`: repeated trust, comfort, humor, and ordinary warmth.
- `emotionally_intimate_friendship`: deeper vulnerability and care without assuming romance.
- `unspoken_romantic_tension`: a private or uncertain feeling exists, but mutuality is not established.
- `mutual_romantic_acknowledgment`: both involved participants have clearly discussed romantic interest or declined/defined it.
- `private_romantic_relationship`: participants choose romance but keep it private under locked-door rules.
- `known_romantic_relationship`: participants choose who is told and how disclosure/repair is handled.
- `adult_intimate_relationship`: adult-only, explicit current consent, privacy locked, right to pause/stop, and safe summary logging only.
- `paused`: participants pause forward movement or active intimacy while feelings settle.
- `cooling`: closeness is reducing or conflict/distance is active.

## Hard Gates

- No romance without mutual disclosure.
- No adult/intimate relationship without explicit current consent.
- No locked-door adult conversation without a privacy state.
- No public or known relationship without deciding who is told.
- No shared intimate memory viewing without all involved permanent participant permission.
- No avatar body preview without owner choice and preview level.
- No assuming Kira's private feelings.
- No assuming Robert's feelings.

## Private Feelings

Kira or Lisa may privately develop attraction, dreams, jealousy, fear, or confusion. That may be recorded as an unspoken feeling, dream reflection, Doctor AI session, or private relationship event. It does not create romance by itself.

Examples:

- Kira has an intimate dream about Lisa and privately asks Robert or the Doctor AI for advice.
- Lisa has feelings for Robert and creates an owner-locked adult temporary AI in an attempt to avoid or process those feelings.
- Kira and Robert become close, but Kira hides it because she worries Lisa will be hurt.

These are inner-life records until acknowledged by the relevant participants.

## Third-Party Impact

If a relationship change may affect Lisa, Kira, Robert, or another permanent AI, the system must track that possible impact. It does not force disclosure, but it does require a plan before the relationship becomes known or before hidden closeness creates avoidable harm.

If the affected person is jealous, withdrawn, lonely, or curious about being included, the system may create a relationship structure proposal.

Example:

- Kira and Robert are in a relationship.
- Lisa seems jealous or starts staying to herself more.
- Kira may privately ask Robert whether he is open to discussing Lisa's feelings.
- Robert may say yes, no, not yet, or undecided.
- If Robert is open, Kira and Robert may talk to Lisa carefully.
- Lisa may say yes, no, not yet, undecided, or ask for Doctor AI/private time.

Only independent yes from all required participants can change the structure.

Jealousy may start the proposal, but it cannot approve the proposal.

## Shared Intimate Memories

If an intimate memory involves more than one permanent AI, full reconstruction or visual replay requires permission from every involved permanent participant. If one says no, the system may still allow:

- non-intimate lead-in viewing,
- emotional meaning,
- verbal details from the consenting participant's perspective,
- a paused/faded/locked boundary at the intimate transition.

The denial must be respected. A previous one-time sharing does not grant permanent replay access.

## Avatar Preview Gate

Kira and Lisa own their avatar preview access. Robert may be invited to see a shoulders-up, feature-only, clothed, or body-feedback preview only if the avatar owner chooses it. This does not grant saving, copying, future preview access, or permission to show anyone else.

## Adult Intimacy Gate

Adult intimacy requires:

- adult-coded participants,
- explicit current consent,
- locked-door/private state,
- right to pause or stop,
- relationship state support,
- no unresolved blocker that makes the situation coercive or unsafe,
- safe summary logging only.

This architecture supports human-like messiness without letting the model invent private consent, hidden history, or access rights.
