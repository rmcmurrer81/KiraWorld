## Natural variation and neutral versus diagnostic states

Medical sources describe substantial normal variation in the size, length,
shape, color, symmetry, and prominence of the mons, clitoral hood/glans,
labia, and vaginal opening. R18 must use plausible slight asymmetry and
regional color/roughness variation without treating one configuration as the
only normal form or using discoloration to fake missing geometry.

In the **natural neutral state**, the hips and thighs are relaxed, the labial
folds rest naturally, and internal vestibular landmarks may be only partly
visible. The model must not enlarge or rotate every landmark toward the
camera merely to make a checklist readable.

In the separate **diagnostic medical-review state**, hip/knee positioning and
gentle labial separation may expose the labia minora, vestibule, urethral
meatus, vaginal introitus, and fourchette for inspection. This state changes
pose and a bounded review control only; it must not replace topology, create a
second anatomy object, permanently distort neutral geometry, or imply an
internal speculum examination. The body must return exactly to neutral.

## Nail implementation implications

The nail plate is a thin keratin plate bonded to the nail bed, curved along
both longitudinal and transverse axes, embedded at the proximal and lateral
nail folds, and joined to the proximal fold by the eponychium/cuticle. R18
nails must therefore conform to each digit's bed, have a subtle proximal
cuticle transition and tapered sidewalls, remain thin and smoothly shaded,
and avoid gaps, floating edges, or intersections. The great-toe plate should
be a broad, gently curved rounded rectangle; smaller plates must be fitted and
scaled individually.
