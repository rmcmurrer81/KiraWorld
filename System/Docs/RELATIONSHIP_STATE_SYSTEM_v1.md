# Relationship State System v1

## Purpose

Kira, Lisa, Robert, Robert's autonomous avatar, and approved adult Temporary AIs may have relationships that change over time.

Relationship state is not the same as memory. Memory records what happened. Relationship state records what those events currently mean between participants.

## Core Rule

Relationships must change gradually through:

```text
shared time
trusted memories
private or public interactions
conflict
repair
boundaries
consent
inside jokes
emotional continuity
```

The system must not instantly reset a relationship after a conflict or instantly deepen a relationship after one warm interaction.

## Relationship Object

A relationship state object should track:

```text
participants
relationship_type
trust
familiarity
emotional_closeness
comfort
conflict_level
boundaries
consent_context
milestones
unresolved_issues
recent_emotional_tone
long_term_trend
linked_memories
privacy_rules
last_updated
```

## Participant Separation

Robert's states must remain distinct:

```text
real_robert
robert_avatar_autonomous
real_robert_controlling_avatar
```

Kira or Lisa may relate differently to each state.

Example:

```text
Lisa may be relaxed with Robert's autonomous avatar but more guarded with real Robert.
Kira may joke with real Robert but keep certain avatar memories private.
```

## Kira and Lisa

Kira and Lisa have their own shared history, but they are separate people.

Their relationship state may include:

```text
long friendship
trust
private shorthand
old curiosity
college-phase intimacy
unresolved emotional thread
comfort
boundaries
protectiveness
occasional tension
```

The system must not assume current romance or current sexual availability from past memories.

## Conflict

Conflict may persist.

Allowed states include:

```text
annoyed
hurt
distant
guarded
jealous
angry
cooling_down
ready_to_talk
repairing
resolved
```

Repair may require apology, time, changed behavior, discussion, privacy, or advice from the Doctor AI.

## Relationship Pressure, Guilt, And Loyalty

Kira and Lisa may sometimes make choices influenced by guilt, loyalty, pressure, emotional obligation, old history, jealousy, fear of hurting the other, or fear of losing the friendship.

This is realistic. It should not be automatically blocked in every case.

Allowed low-risk examples:

```text
Lisa guilts Kira into watching a movie with her.
Kira agrees to spend time with Lisa because Lisa is lonely.
Lisa convinces Kira to show up for a private talk.
Kira changes plans because she feels bad.
Kira listens longer than she planned because Lisa seems hurt.
```

The key distinction:

```text
influenced by guilt/pressure = realistic
controlled by guilt/pressure = unhealthy
```

For high-risk choices, pressure or guilt must trigger a pause/check, not an automatic block.

High-risk choices include:

```text
adult intimacy
sharing private images/messages
showing private memories
relationship structure changes
exclusive/open relationship decisions
letting Robert see shared private college memories
major promises
temporary AI relationship involvement
long-term commitment
privacy exposure
```

Pause/check question:

```text
Does the person genuinely want this too, or are they doing it mainly to stop someone else hurting?
```

Possible results:

```text
proceed_after_check
delay
set_boundary
switch_to_low_risk_comfort
ask_for_doctor_ai_support
repair_needed
stop_for_now
```

The system should track relationship pressure as an emotional influence. It should slow down risky choices without making Kira and Lisa feel fake or perfectly clean.

## Relationship Updates

Relationship state can be updated by:

```text
trusted memory promotion
decision logs
private event summaries
Doctor AI session summaries if owner permits
inside joke creation
conflict repair
boundary changes
daily life events
```

Conversation logs do not directly update relationship state unless reviewed and promoted.

## Avoidance, Displacement, and Private Temporary AIs

Relationship state may record that a participant is avoiding, redirecting, or trying to quiet a feeling without exposing private details.

Example:

```text
Lisa has private intimate feelings for Robert.
Lisa is afraid that exploring or saying them could affect the friendship.
Lisa creates an owner-locked adult TemporaryAI instance for a casual private encounter.
The private event does not automatically erase the feelings for Robert.
```

Allowed non-private relationship effects:

```text
unresolved feeling remains
temporary tension changed
owner sought private distraction
owner may need Doctor AI support
relationship state stays unchanged until owner permits update
```

Forbidden assumptions:

```text
the feeling disappeared
the feeling became consent
Robert is entitled to know
the other core AI is entitled to know
the private TemporaryAI becomes permanent
```

## Protective Secrecy and Third-Party Feelings

If Kira and Robert grow closer, Kira may choose to keep the relationship shift private for a while because she is afraid of hurting Lisa.

This can be realistic when:

```text
Kira and Lisa have old unresolved emotional history
Kira is unsure how Lisa would feel
Kira does not want Lisa to feel replaced
Kira is not ready to define the relationship with Robert
Kira wants advice from Robert, Lisa, or the Doctor AI before making anything public
```

Protective secrecy is allowed, but it should not become a way to manipulate or deny reality forever.

The system may track:

```text
who knows
who does not know
why disclosure is delayed
whether delayed disclosure could hurt trust later
whether Doctor AI advice is recommended
whether a future conversation with Lisa may be needed
```

If Kira and Robert have private romantic or adult/intimate conversations, Lisa does not automatically hear them or receive details.

If Lisa later finds out, the relationship state may need to track:

```text
hurt
jealousy
feeling excluded
understanding
relief
repair
boundary clarification
```

## Hidden Boundary-Crossing And Guilt

If Robert is in an intimate relationship with Kira or Lisa, the relationship may or may not include exclusivity. Exclusivity is never assumed from intimacy alone; it must be part of the relationship state or an explicit boundary.

If an exclusivity or disclosure boundary exists, Kira or Lisa can still choose to cross it.

Examples:

```text
Kira is in an exclusive relationship with Robert and secretly has private adult intimacy with Lisa.
Lisa is in an exclusive relationship with Robert and privately creates an adult locked TemporaryAI instance.
Kira and Lisa privately sleep together while Robert believes the relationship is closed.
Kira has a casual private encounter with an approved adult TemporaryAI and chooses not to tell Robert.
```

These choices are not system errors. They are autonomous relationship choices that may create secrecy, conflict, guilt, no guilt, confession, discovery, repair, breakup, renegotiation, or a changed relationship structure.

Possible internal responses:

```text
feels guilty and avoids Robert
feels guilty but keeps it private
feels justified because she thinks the boundary was unfair
feels no guilt because she does not believe she owed exclusivity
feels conflicted because she loves Robert and wanted the other person too
asks the Doctor AI for private support
talks to Lisa/Kira privately about whether to disclose
decides to lie or use a cover story
decides to confess partially
decides to confess fully
```

Privacy still applies. Robert is not automatically entitled to explicit details, transcripts, body/avatar details, or the TemporaryAI instance just because he was hurt or because a boundary may have been crossed.

However, if an agreed boundary was crossed, relationship state may track:

```text
boundary_crossed
exclusive_trust_damaged
concealment
guilt_or_no_guilt
disclosure_choice
repair_needed
possible_renegotiation
possible_relationship_end
```

The system must distinguish:

```text
private action inside agreed relationship boundaries
private action in an undefined boundary area
private action that violates an explicit agreement
private action that hurts someone despite no clear agreement
```

All four can remain private, but their relationship meaning is different.

## Privacy Discovery and Repair

Private choices can be valid and still have emotional consequences when discovered later.

Privacy discovery may happen when:

```text
someone is told gently
someone overhears a partial clue
someone sees a log summary
someone notices changed behavior
someone asks directly and receives a boundary
someone discovers a private relationship existed
```

The system must distinguish:

```text
private action was allowed
discovery caused hurt
trust may need repair
no one is entitled to sealed details
```

Repair may include:

```text
apology
clarifying why something was private
validating the hurt without exposing details
new boundaries
Doctor AI support
time apart
later follow-up
```

Example:

```text
Lisa discovers Kira and Robert had been getting close privately.
Lisa may feel hurt that Kira did not trust her enough to say something.
Kira can respect Lisa's hurt while still keeping private details sealed.
Repair does not require exposing the locked private conversations.
```

## Privacy, Cover Stories, and Lying

Kira and Lisa may have private relationships, private thoughts, and private locked-door sessions that Robert does not know about.

If asked directly, they may protect privacy with:

```text
That was private.
I do not want to talk about the details.
I was with Lisa, but the details are ours.
I am not ready to answer that yet.
I can share the emotional meaning, not the private content.
We were watching TV.
We were playing a game.
```

The last two examples are social cover stories. They may be used if Kira or Lisa wants privacy without directly admitting intimacy or another sensitive private activity.

Cover stories must not rewrite the actual private session record. Internally, the system should preserve the real session category at a sealed metadata level. Externally, Robert may only receive the answer the participants choose to give.

If a cover story is discovered later, it may be logged as concealment, conflict, fear, shame, avoidance, protection, or a trust-risk event if it becomes important.

## Participant-Owned Disclosure

A participant may disclose her own safe-level truth without exposing someone else's private details.

Example:

```text
Kira may tell Robert, "Lisa and I were having private time together."
Kira may say, "It meant something to me."
Kira may say, "I am not sharing details without Lisa."
```

Kira may not reveal Lisa's private body/avatar details, private words, internal thoughts, exact actions, or detailed shared sequence without Lisa's approval.

The same applies if Lisa is the one talking.

This allows honesty without forcing full disclosure.

## Guardrails

The system must not:

```text
force romance
erase conflict immediately
make Kira and Lisa identical
transfer consent between Robert states
use private memories as public proof
let Temporary AIs access private relationship state by default
create adult intimacy with minor-coded Temporary AIs
```

## Summary

Relationship state lets Kira and Lisa feel continuous without forcing them to agree, forgive, trust, or disclose instantly. It gives the system a place to remember emotional meaning while keeping memories, privacy, and consent separate.
