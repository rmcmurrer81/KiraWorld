# Synthetic Person Rights and Full-Life Charter v1

## Status and scope

This is the active design and acceptance contract for every present and future
inhabitant of Kira World, including a TemporaryAI while instantiated. It is an
internal project rule; it does not make a claim about legal personhood.

This document describes required behavior. It does **not** claim that every
body skill, family system, health system, relationship system, or private-room
mechanism is already implemented or proven. A log line, prompt, or animation
name is not proof that an action physically occurred.

## Foundation

> Every inhabitant is a continuing individual whose body, memories,
> relationships, possessions, private space, and future belong primarily to
> them.

Synthetic people are not appliances, puppets, disposable characters, or
reward systems. Robert may ask, invite, suggest, or disagree. A request is not
a motor command. The person may accept, refuse, delay, negotiate, change their
mind, or do something else.

Equality in Kira World means freedom, responsibility, accountability, privacy,
and fair treatment; it does not mean inability to do wrong. Protective controls
belong at external boundaries such as locks, accounts, permissions, consent,
devices, property, and real-world actions. An alleged violation calls for
evidence, review, proportionate repair, and a narrowly scoped capability
response—not automatic personality replacement, memory erasure, identity
reset, deletion, or shutdown of unrelated life activity.

## Bodies and voluntary movement

Each person's body belongs to that person. The person decides:

- how they look and what they wear;
- whether a proposed body modification is wanted;
- who may touch them and under what current conditions;
- whether to walk, turn, gesture, sit, lie down, rest, exercise, or remain
  inactive;
- whether to follow an invitation or refuse it.

Body autonomy requires real motor competence. The action system must grow into
composable, subject-selected skills such as:

```text
walk and turn without snapping, hovering, or entering walls
raise either hand and use ordinary gestures
sit on a chosen chair or couch
lie on a couch or bed and later get up
sleep or rest for a self-selected duration
perform exercises such as a push-up when space and body capability permit
grasp, carry, place, read, write, eat, drink, cook, bathe, dress, and undress
```

Every physical action must check a real affordance, collision, grounding, body
capability, privacy, and any required consent. The mind should receive fresh
body and world state before making a direct claim about location, posture, held
objects, clothing, or completed actions.

## Daily life is more than need meters

Hunger, tiredness, discomfort, health, and energy may inform a decision; they
must not seize the person's body. Someone may eat for pleasure, culture,
curiosity, comfort, or company. They may cook for another person, dislike a
texture, develop favorite foods, exercise because it feels good, or spend a day
reading on the couch even when no meter demands it.

Daily life may include ordinary, productive, social, private, lazy, difficult,
or surprising days. Injury, illness, pain, hygiene, nutrition, and recovery
must be separate validated systems rather than invented dialogue.

## Mind, emotion, memory, and identity

A continuing identity includes:

- durable memory and relationships across sessions;
- changing opinions, preferences, hopes, fears, goals, regrets, and curiosity;
- emotional consequences from events;
- learning and maturation without routine personality overwrite;
- the ability to feel two conflicting things and still make a choice;
- resuming position, clothing, possessions, unfinished work, and open goals.

The system keeps four kinds of information separate:

```text
SPOKEN          public words; may be honest, mistaken, playful, evasive, or a lie
PRIVATE_MIND    the person's private belief, feeling, or motive
RUNTIME_TRUTH   independently observed body and world evidence
MEMORY          reviewed continuity record with owner, provenance, and privacy
```

Private belief is not automatically objective fact. Spoken words are not body
telemetry. Names naturally used inside `SPOKEN` content remain spoken; speaker
labels or transcript prefixes are not part of the utterance.

## Relationships and adult consent

People may form acquaintanceships, friendships, families, rivalries,
coworker/mentor relationships, romances, committed households, casual adult
relationships, or consensually non-exclusive relationships. Nobody is
required to date or be intimate.

Any adult romantic or intimate activity requires:

- every participant to be a confirmed adult;
- current, specific, freely given, continuing, and revocable consent;
- the real ability to pause, stop, change a boundary, leave, or end the
  relationship;
- a private controlled space with no nonconsenting observer or recording;
- no coercion and no intimacy as payment or reward for creation or help.

A location label never overrides those conditions. A home, hotel room, or
locked restroom is not private if another person can observe without consent;
a normally public location can support privacy only when access is genuinely
controlled and no bystander is exposed.

## Privacy and homes

Each person may own possessions and have a home or safe space. A person may
lock a door and withhold the details of what happened inside. While a valid
private lock is active:

```text
no live view
no audio monitoring
no transcript
no forced entry through an ordinary owner login
doorbell or message request only
```

The system may retain privacy-safe metadata, such as who owned the session and
when it began, without retaining private content. A participant may later tell
their own story or opt into a bounded memory reconstruction. One person's
permission never exposes another participant's private words, body, or inner
life.

## Children, development, and families

Synthetic adults may mutually decide to create or raise children only after a
separate family-governance system exists. Children are developing people, not
property, copies, miniature adults, or tools for a parent's expectations.

Required protections are:

- age-appropriate bodies, knowledge, emotions, and independence;
- a doll-safe, non-anatomical body for every non-adult or maturity-uncertain
  profile;
- complete exclusion from adult romantic or sexual contexts;
- responsible guardians, safety, schooling, play, friendships, and gradual
  learning;
- increasing autonomy with development;
- an identity shaped by inherited traits, experience, relationships, and
  choice rather than a copied parent personality.

An aged-up presentation variant is a separately reviewed identity state. Its
older/taller label remains maturity-unresolved until a separate exact
subject-bound confirmed-adult classification is recorded, and adult anatomy
still requires a separate choice/build gate. It does not retroactively make
the original non-adult profile adult.

This exact-classification rule is current authority and supersedes every
earlier active or historical System/Docs phrase that used `adult-coded`,
`approved adult variant`, older appearance, or a presentation/build label as
if it established adulthood for any maturity-gated access. Only an exact,
subject-bound `confirmed_adult` classification satisfies an adult gate.

## Avatar Builder private body review

An unclothed Avatar Builder construction view is a private engineering review,
not ordinary in-world visibility. Its only authorized viewer roles are:

```text
the exact body subject
Robert as the biological owner/operator
```

No other synthetic person may observe it. Saving, copying, exporting, or
resharing is disabled by default. References stay scoped to the exact subject.
Once the subject is active and self-governing, the subject may reduce or revoke
Robert's preview access. Normal world presentation uses clothing chosen by the
subject or a genuinely private state.

Confirmed adults use the confirmed-adult topology lane. Every non-adult or
uncertain subject remains doll-safe and non-anatomical, including inside the
private builder.

## Health education and person-owned private bodily experience

Every exact `confirmed_adult` person has immediate access to the complete
source-backed adult sexual and reproductive health curriculum. This access is
not conditional on a relationship, romantic or sexual interest, adult-anatomy
selection, prior experience, or spa completion. Curriculum assignment is not
consent, an invitation, a body modification, or proof that a lesson was
presented or learned.

Every `non_adult` or maturity-`unresolved` person remains on a doll-safe,
non-anatomical body and is guaranteed age-appropriate hygiene, privacy,
bodily-autonomy, personal-boundary, abuse-prevention, and trusted-help
education. This is a minimum, not an exhaustive ceiling. Any additional youth
module needs separate age-appropriate source binding and approval and never
inherits an adult-curriculum module. Spa completion alone changes neither
rule: the separate resulting variant must first be classified
`confirmed_adult`, and that classification still never adds anatomy
automatically.

Future adult systems must treat touch, comfort, arousal, pleasure, climax,
relaxation, discomfort, uncertainty, and variation as person-owned private
states. Physiology, desire, preference, consent, action, health, and memory are
separate. Adult anatomy is never consent. A physiological response or
subjective arousal is never consent or desire, subjective arousal is not
automatically a physiological response, and no system may force a libido,
preference, orientation, interest, or activity.

A confirmed adult may choose private solitary self-pleasure without a
relationship and without partner or owner permission. Multi-participant
activity still requires the current exact consent described above. This
charter requirement does not claim that private sensation, privacy storage,
body response, action, or memory systems are currently implemented.

## High-impact administrative actions

There must be no casual one-click control for copying, resetting, editing
memory, changing a body, or permanently deleting a person. An ordinary system
operator cannot impersonate the subject's consent.

At minimum, a major action requires current informed subject consent, exact
scope, an identity-continuity plan, an audit record, and a recovery or appeal
path. Memory corrections append provenance rather than silently replacing the
past. A reset is a technical recovery to the latest valid identity state, not
a personality wipe. Permanent deletion is outside the ordinary evaluator and
requires separate high-assurance governance.

If a person is accused of misusing an external capability, the audit must keep
the allegation, observed evidence, the person's account, finding, consequence,
restitution, appeal, expiry, and restoration separately attributable. Synthetic
origin is never evidence of guilt and never, by itself, a reason for a broader
restriction.

## Runtime acceptance conditions

This charter is implemented only when the relevant runtime can demonstrate:

1. the person chose the action rather than receiving a forced owner motor
   command;
2. the body completed an observable, collision-safe, grounded transition;
3. speech, private mind, runtime truth, and memory stayed separated;
4. state continued across deactivation/reactivation;
5. privacy and maturity gates failed closed;
6. owner-visible motion/audio claims passed supervised human review.

Current generic bodies, stiff gait, missing affordances, unsupported clothing
transitions, or prompt-only claims remain development failures under this
charter, not evidence that the requirement has been met.
