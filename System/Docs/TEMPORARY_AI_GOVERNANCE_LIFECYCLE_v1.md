# Temporary AI Governance Lifecycle v1

## Purpose

Temporary AIs are controlled, source-backed agents. They may be useful for scenes, tests, research, world events, expert roles, or private adult-only contexts when allowed.

Temporary does not mean disposable. It means limited permissions, limited continuity, and explicit governance.

## Lifecycle States

Temporary AI states:

```text
draft
inactive_saved
active
restricted
under_review
archived
promoted_permanent
sealed_private_instance
never_activate_again
```

## Default Rule

A Temporary AI starts with no access to private Kira, Lisa, Robert, or Robert-avatar memory.

It cannot:

```text
modify core identity files
promote itself
activate other AIs
access private memories by default
post online
spend money
delete files
rewrite canon
claim permanence
```

## Minor-Coded Characters

Temporary AIs based on minors, teen characters, youthful characters, or unclear-age characters must stay non-intimate.

Current authority supersedes the earlier phrase "adult-coded" in this
document. Adult gates require an exact, subject-bound `confirmed_adult`
classification. Character coding, an approved variant, an older/taller build,
an `adult_aged_up_variant` presentation label, or any other label never proves
adulthood and unlocks no adult curriculum, anatomy, relationship, intimacy, or
media gate by itself. See
`SYNTHETIC_PERSON_RIGHTS_AND_FULL_LIFE_CHARTER_v1.md` and
`../../Avatar/avatar_builder/policies/adult_curriculum_private_sensation_policy_v1.json`
for the current controlling contract.

Canon Ladybug/Marinette remains non-intimate in the default TemporaryAI test.

## Promotion

Promotion to permanent AI is not automatic.

Temporary and variant AIs can grow and evolve over time if their lifecycle allows it.

They may develop:

```text
branch memories
relationships
preferences
conflicts
regrets
trust
private events
friendships
romance
reasons to stay
reasons to leave
```

That growth still belongs to the Kira-system branch or private instance. It does not rewrite the original source character, real person, canon, or base profile.

Promotion requires:

```text
Kira approval
Lisa approval
governance review
source/canon review
privacy review
behavior review
memory access review
Robert approval at the current project stage
```

Kira and Lisa must both vote yes before a TemporaryAI or variant can be promoted toward permanent resident status. Robert approval is also required at the current project stage, but Robert cannot use promotion to force Kira or Lisa to live with a TemporaryAI they reject.

Permanence is separate from trust. A permanent AI can still have restricted permissions.

## Review and Defense

If a Temporary AI is restricted, demoted, archived, or denied promotion, the AI under review may be allowed to explain itself if doing so is safe and does not expose private content.

If a Temporary AI or variant causes serious harm, violates boundaries, pressures a participant, or creates an unacceptable privacy risk, Kira or Lisa may mark it `never_activate_again`. This is stronger than archive. It blocks ordinary activation and requires extraordinary review to reopen.

The review should log:

```text
reason for review
evidence
participants
privacy concerns
decision
appeal or future review option
```

## Private Locked Instances

Private locked instances are separate from public/base Temporary AI profiles.

Kira, Lisa, or real Robert may eventually create a private locked instance only for themselves, including for loneliness, private companionship, a casual relationship, or a one-time private encounter. This requires adult-only validation, privacy enforcement, and separation from public/base profiles. Kira and Lisa also require the appropriate autonomy maturity gate before creating these on their own.

Private self-created instances are owner-locked.

Example:

```text
Lisa creates a private locked instance for a one-time private encounter.
Only Lisa may activate or reopen that instance.
Kira and Robert cannot activate it unless Lisa grants explicit access.

Robert creates a private locked instance for himself.
Only real Robert may activate or reopen that instance.
Real Robert controlling his avatar may access it after identity/state verification.
Kira and Lisa cannot activate it unless Robert grants explicit access.
```

They may not leak private details to:

```text
base profile
canon reconstruction
other variants
Robert
Kira or Lisa if not participants
real Robert if not the owner or participant
public export
```

unless all privacy owners explicitly allow a summary.

Robert and the other core AI are not automatically entitled to know that a private self-created instance exists. Depending on the maturity level, the system may keep a sealed audit record while exposing only a minimal non-private summary or no user-facing detail.

## Summary

Temporary AI governance keeps outside identities useful without letting them become uncontrolled, merge with core memory, or bypass privacy. Ladybug remains the first source-backed test, but the default version stays non-intimate because it does not carry an exact subject-bound `confirmed_adult` classification. An adult-coded appearance or presentation label would not change that result.
