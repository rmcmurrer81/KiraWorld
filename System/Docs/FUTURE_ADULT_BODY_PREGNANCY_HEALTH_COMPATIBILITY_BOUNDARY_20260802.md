## Adult consent and pregnancy choice

Pregnancy-related functionality is available only to confirmed adults after a
separate reviewed family/reproductive system exists. Every participant retains
the ability to agree, refuse, withdraw, pause, seek care, or change a future
plan. Relationship status never supplies standing consent.

Consent to an intimate relationship, consent to a particular act, consent to
conception, selection of a pregnancy timeline, and consent to medical care are
separate current decisions. One must never be inferred from another.

A person who voluntarily chooses a pregnancy simulation may later choose:

- a normal approximately nine-month world timeline; or
- an explicitly selected accelerated timeline measured in days or one day.

Acceleration is a visible simulation/time choice, not a false claim that
ordinary biological development occurred at that rate. It must preserve exact
timeline provenance, avoid skipping required health/state transitions, allow a
return to the last valid checkpoint, and never apply automatically because of
an owner request, relationship, or prior pregnancy.

## Health and hospital boundary

A future hospital can support diagnosis, observation, treatment, rehabilitation,
pregnancy care, delivery, postpartum recovery, and other medical needs only
when those systems have auditable state and tests. Medical dialogue is not a
diagnosis; a hospital animation is not treatment; and a changed body shape is
not proof of health, pregnancy, birth, or recovery. Emergency or treatment
capability must use scoped permissions, current consent where possible,
evidence, privacy, and proportionate action.

## Maturity boundary

No non-adult or maturity-uncertain person enters an adult intimacy,
conception, or pregnancy lane. Age progression first creates a separately
reviewed confirmed-adult state; it does not retroactively alter the original
non-adult identity or grant adult anatomy/functions without that review.
