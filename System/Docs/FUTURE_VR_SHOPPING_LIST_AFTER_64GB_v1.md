# Future VR Shopping List After 64 GB v1

Status: deferred research list only. Nothing in this file authorizes a purchase,
installation, VR activation, or a second live 3D resident.

## Purchase Gate

Do not buy either headset yet. Revisit this list only after:

1. The desktop has at least 64 GB of stable RAM, preferably a matched kit.
2. The new memory passes Windows Memory Diagnostic or an equivalent extended
   memory test at the intended speed.
3. Kira passes a supervised multi-hour Home World, voice, and memory soak.
4. The current RTX 5060 Ti 16 GB passes a measured VR render test at the chosen
   headset's resolution without persistent stutter or VRAM exhaustion.
5. Current KAT, glove, haptic-suit, SteamVR/OpenXR, and headset compatibility is
   checked again immediately before purchase.

Prices are deliberately not frozen here because the purchase is deferred.

## Headset Candidates

### Candidate A — VIVE Focus Vision

Current role: leading expansion-oriented candidate.

Why it remains on the list:

- Hybrid standalone and PC VR operation.
- Optional DisplayPort mode through the separately sold Wired Streaming Kit.
- Built-in eye and hand tracking.
- Optional face and body tracking ecosystem.
- SteamVR route for the future Kira World client.

Items to price together later:

- VIVE Focus Vision headset and controllers.
- VIVE Wired Streaming Kit for DisplayPort PC VR.
- Only after software proof: Facial Tracker for VIVE Focus Series.
- Only after software proof: VIVE Ultimate Trackers and Wireless Dongle.

Official reference: [VIVE Focus Vision overview](https://www.vive.com/us/product/vive-focus-vision/overview/).

### Candidate B — Meta Quest 3 512 GB

Current role: lower-cost/value comparison candidate.

Why it remains on the list:

- Standalone operation and broad consumer software support.
- PC connection through Meta Horizon Link.
- Useful lower-cost entry point if the later Kira World PC-VR tests do not need
  the Focus Vision tracking expansion path.

Items to price together later:

- Meta Quest 3 512 GB headset and controllers.
- Compatible wired Link cable or a measured Wi-Fi setup, but not both by
  default.
- Comfort strap/battery only after Robert tries the standard fit.

Official references: [Meta Quest 3](https://www.meta.com/quest/quest-3/) and
[Meta Horizon Link](https://www.meta.com/help/quest/1517439565442928/).

## Later Accessories — Do Not Buy With The First Headset

These remain separate later phases:

- KAT Walk locomotion system.
- Tracked gloves.
- Haptic body suit.
- Extra body trackers.
- Face tracker.
- Replaceable hygiene interfaces and prescription-lens solution if needed.

Each accessory needs a Kira World input test, an emergency-stop test, a comfort
test, and a fresh manufacturer compatibility check. KAT's current compatibility
page is [here](https://www.kat-vr.com/pages/kat-headset-compatibility/).

shopping action is `wait`.
