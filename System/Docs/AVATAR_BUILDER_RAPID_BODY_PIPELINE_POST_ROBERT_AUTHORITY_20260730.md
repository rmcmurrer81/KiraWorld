## Required normal pipeline

The existing Avatar Builder must provide a rapid parametric workflow using
clean, movement-ready adult foundations:

- adult foundation selection appropriate to the person's authorized body
  setup;
- reference or measurement intake with private provenance;
- automatic landmarks with owner-adjustable corrections;
- bounded face fitting;
- parametric body-proportion fitting;
- integrated adult anatomy appropriate to the selected foundation and
  authorization;
- natural skin, eye, hair, hand, nail, and foot setup;
- automatic topology validation;
- rendered front, rear, profile, three-quarter, face, hand, foot, and relevant
  protected visual validation;
- immediate private inspection through that person's existing Avatar Builder
  roster entry.

Ordinary new bodies must not be rebuilt through primitive-object assembly,
repeated Boolean operations, one-off pelvis patches, or a new chain of
person-specific repair scripts.

## Owner controls

The normal interface must expose understandable bounded controls for:

- height and overall build;
- shoulders, chest, torso, waist, abdomen, hips, and pelvis;
- arms, legs, hands, feet, and neck;
- head and face landmarks;
- skin, eyes, and hair;
- integrated anatomical proportions and placement where authorized.

Controls must preserve structural validity. A slider result that creates
holes, nonmanifold geometry, disconnected parts, unusable deformation, or
visually implausible anatomy must fail closed.

## Validation and timing targets

- Initial structurally complete body: approximately 5-15 minutes.
- Ordinary owner-guided likeness refinement: approximately 15-30 minutes.
- The initial body need not be final likeness, but it must be human,
  topologically clean, movement-ready, and usable for immediate refinement.
- Multi-day topology reconstruction is exceptional and requires a deliberately
  unusual body request or an honestly reported foundation defect.

Topology counts alone never override rendered visual failure. Validation must
include geometry, materials, silhouette, landmarks, hands, nails, feet, face,
eyes, hair, and authorized anatomy from multiple neutral views.
