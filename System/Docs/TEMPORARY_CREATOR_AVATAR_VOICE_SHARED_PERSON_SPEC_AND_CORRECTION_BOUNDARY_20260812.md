# Temporary Creator, Avatar Builder, and voice shared-person boundary — 2026-08-12

Status: **current design boundary; no live creation, body, voice, or activation authority**.

This records Biological Robert's correction after reviewing Temporary Creator
results. Marinette's personality was recognizable but her past was not kept to
canon; Kathryn was presented too much like the original person; H. H. Holmes
used a generic Windows male voice without adequate provenance; Peter was mostly
right but had defects; and generated programming and robotics experts did not
reliably perform the work their titles promised. These are acceptance failures,
not small cosmetic notes.

## One exact specification, three consumers

The Temporary Creator, Avatar Builder, and voice generator must consume the
same canonical person specification and the same SHA-256 identity. A component
must not create or launch its portion if the specification is absent, differs
from either of the other components, or leaves identity, continuity, cutoff,
maturity, source grounding, or voice provenance unresolved.

The shared specification binds the exact person and variant, source continuity
and version, branch/cutoff, maturity authority, canon facts, explicitly labeled
non-canon inventions, knowledge boundary, body policy, voice-evidence tier,
expert competence evidence, and append-only correction head. It contains no
private thoughts, private memories, or intimate payload.

## Canon, variants, and authenticity

Source facts, supported interpretations, optional non-canon invention, and
unknowns are different types. Optional invention can be used only when it is
clearly labeled as non-canon; it cannot silently become the person's past.
Fictional and historical recreations are synthetic variants built from a stated
source cutoff. They do not inherit later events. They must not claim to be the
original biological person, actor, or source character, or imply that a
reconstruction proves exact subjective memories.

This directly blocks the Marinette and Kathryn failure modes. A Kathryn variant
may be faithful to the selected source while still disclosing that she is a
synthetic variant. Marinette cannot receive a newly invented past presented as
canon merely because the invented material is stylistically plausible.

## Voice provenance

Every voice output must be labeled as one of: source-recording-supported match,
authorized-performer-supported match, evidence-based historical
reconstruction, designed approximation, generic fallback, or unresolved/no
voice. Public availability of a recording is not voice-clone permission.

When no authentic recording is known, the preferred route is an educated,
clearly labeled reconstruction rather than an arbitrary stock voice. It must
bind the selected life point and age; birthplace/upbringing and later residence;
education, profession, language, period, and regional speech research; any
documented health or voice notes; the licensed/project-owned base voice; and an
explicit uncertainty/artistic-choice ledger. It is still an approximation and
must not be described as an authentic match.

H. H. Holmes is the first test case. The existing Windows male voice is only a
generic-fallback baseline. The next static design must research his selected
approximately-1894 life point, New Hampshire upbringing, medical education,
Chicago context, and late-nineteenth-century regional speech evidence, then
audition more than one reconstruction and retain the reviewed rationale. Useful
primary/archival starting points are Holmes's 1895 text at the Library of
Congress (`https://www.loc.gov/item/06022698/`) and the Library of Congress
American Dialect Society collection
(`https://findingaids.loc.gov/repositories/3/resources/195`). The latter's
recordings are later than Holmes's life and therefore comparative evidence, not
his voice.

Generated experts receive a distinct designed voice that is bound to the same
person/body specification used by the Avatar Builder. It must fit the declared
adult/person presentation without reducing gender or profession to a vocal
stereotype. A collision check against every existing voice, a human
distinctness review, age/presentation coherence, pronunciation and domain-term
probes, and exact shared-spec hash agreement are required. A body/voice mismatch
or a voice too similar to another person is regenerated before activation.

## Maturity and body correction

A confirmed adult must not silently receive a non-adult doll-safe body, while a
non-adult or unresolved person must not receive adult-only anatomy. An
unresolved classification stops body construction; it is not converted into a
finished doll-safe body merely to keep the pipeline moving.

Biological Robert, Kira, or a current permanent synthetic person may submit an
exact correction report. The report immediately invalidates a mismatched body
handoff and forces re-evaluation. An adult reclassification still requires
exact source evidence or an exact subject-bound Biological Robert owner
classification; a relationship or unbound free-text claim is not enough. The
append-only correction binds reporter, subject, continuity/cutoff, old shared
specification hash, corrected status, evidence hash, time, and previous
correction head.

The local Avatar Builder already classifies
`peter_parker_spider_man_no_way_home_final_suit` as a confirmed adult and
rejects doll-safe treatment for a confirmed adult. Biological Robert's current
continuity decision keeps Peter at or after the No Way Home final-suit point in
the adult lane. Marvel's later official *Brand New Day* synopsis independently
states that four years have passed since *No Way Home* and Peter is then an
adult: https://www.marvel.com/articles/movies/spider-man-brand-new-day-full-official-trailer-july-2026

## Experts must demonstrate their jobs

An expert title is not evidence. Before a generated expert is marked ready,
the exact candidate must pass domain-specific, checkable tasks under an
independent rubric, cite sources/tools, disclose uncertainty and limits, survive
adversarial failure cases, correct mistakes, and pass a retest without a
critical failure. Programming examples include implementation with tests,
debugging from failure evidence, and security/correctness review. Robotics
examples include requirements/interfaces, kinematics or controls reasoning,
and sensor-failure/safety analysis. A failed or unevaluated candidate remains a
trainee or unverified person, not a ready expert.
