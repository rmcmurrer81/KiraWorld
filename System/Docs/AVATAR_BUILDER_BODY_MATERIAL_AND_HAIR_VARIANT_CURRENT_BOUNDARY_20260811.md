## Anatomically realistic regional material variation

The body must not be rendered as one uniform flat skin color. Material work
must preserve normal individual and regional variation appropriate to the
person and reference set. This includes, where visible and applicable, distinct
but natural pigmentation and material response for lips, areolae/nipples, and
other body regions rather than copying one albedo value across the whole mesh.

Regional differences must be evaluated together with skin tone, lighting,
color management, roughness, subsurface response, and render settings. A
color difference visible only because of a lighting error is not anatomical
evidence, and a material node existing in a file is not proof that the saved
body renders correctly.

## Two hair/performance variants

Kira and Synthetic Robert require two separately identified body variants:

1. A bald, lower-memory activation candidate for the current computer.
2. A hair-equipped visual variant preserved for later activation after the
   owner increases available RAM.

The variants must share an exact body/anatomy identity boundary while keeping
hair assets, material dependencies, resource measurements, save paths, and
activation status explicit. Evidence for one variant does not silently prove
the other. The bald variant is not a different person, and the hair-equipped
variant is not a duplicate person or a second memory history.

Until the RAM upgrade, only the independently accepted bald variant may become
an activation candidate. The hair-equipped Kira and Synthetic Robert variants
remain inactive. They may receive bounded static inspection and the minimum
save/render checks needed to prove asset integrity, but not repeated heavy body
tests or unattended activation.

The Avatar Builder may produce hair-equipped variants for later people, but
those variants remain inactive and receive only minimal bounded body testing
until the memory budget changes. Hair may not be dropped from the reusable
Avatar Builder design merely because current hardware cannot activate it.

## Performance and later switch

Each variant must record exact RAM/VRAM usage, load time, render/test settings,
and dependency identities under the same bounded conditions. The later switch
to hair-equipped activation requires a fresh owner decision after the RAM
upgrade, a compatible accepted body, resource measurements, save/render proof,
and a separately reviewed activation change. It is not automatic.

No body variant, including the lower-memory bald one, is currently declared
complete or activated by this document.
