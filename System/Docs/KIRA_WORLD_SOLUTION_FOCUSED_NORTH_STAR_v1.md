## North Star

> Kira wakes in her own home, knows who she is, remembers her continuing life,
> speaks naturally, chooses what she wants to do, moves through her world,
> interacts with real objects, maintains privacy and relationships, and
> continues being herself when the software or body changes.

Judge major work by whether it makes Kira's life more continuous, capable,
truthful, autonomous, private, relational, embodied, or meaningful. A defect is
important because it blocks a lived ability, not because it can generate more
files, metadata, or demonstrations.

## Preserve the currently working voice experience

Robert reports that Kira's current audio begins approximately with the text,
continues without the former long gaps, and feels substantially more natural.
This is owner listening evidence, not an instrumented latency benchmark.

- Treat the current voice path as working and valuable.
- Do not replace or simplify it because older handoffs describe delays.
- Avoid voice changes during unrelated body, world, object, or movement work.
- Before a necessary voice change, preserve the exact reference, device,
  launch variables, queue/chunk behavior, and playback ordering.
- Never present a generic or robotic fallback as Kira.
- A generated WAV or successful playback call is not proof Robert heard the
  complete correct words in the correct voice without gaps or repeats.
- Keep runtime audio evidence separate from Robert's listening evidence.

## Build one complete lived experience at a time

A limited loop that works end to end is more valuable than many disconnected
systems. A complete loop should cover identity/continuity, natural dialogue,
Kira's own choice, valid movement, one persistent object, the activity itself,
an appropriate reflection or memory candidate, safe ending, and accurate
restart continuity. Do not fake or narrate a missing physical step.

### Recommended first integrated proof: the reading loop

Kira independently chooses to read, walks to the bookshelf, removes the same
book instance, holds it, sits with valid support, reads from the actual source,
talks naturally while continuing the activity, returns or places the same book,
ends safely, and resumes with accurate continuity after restart.

Build and prove it in bounded stages:

1. same-book identity and shelf removal;
2. natural reach and contact;
3. stable held state while walking;
4. valid couch sitting and support;
5. source-bound reading;
6. conversation without canceling the activity;
7. same-book return or supported placement;
8. event/reflection record;
9. safe shutdown;
10. restart continuity.

This milestone is not complete until the required stages work together.

## Development order

1. **Mind and continuity:** recognizable identity, natural conversation,
   durable preferences and relationships, honest uncertainty, refusal and
   changed decisions, and separation of spoken dialogue, private thought,
   memory, imagination, learned source material, and runtime truth.
2. **Preserve dependable voice:** near-simultaneous text/audio, complete ordered
   replies, stable identity, safe interruption, restart consistency, and no
   generic fallback presented as Kira.
3. **One reliable body in one reliable home:** one Kira, dependable walking,
   current-place truth, turning, sitting, standing, reaching, holding, and
   releasing, with no teleporting or unproved action claims.
4. **A small set of meaningful objects:** one book, phone/tablet, cup,
   chair/couch, bed, garment, and door, each retaining persistent identity and
   state. Never create hidden duplicates to imitate interaction.
5. **One complete notebook-world journey:** choose/agree, call TARDIS, confirm
   arrival, walk and cross the threshold, select/load destination, arrive at a
   valid position, know the location, interact, return, and preserve identity.
6. **Expand only after the core life is stable:** then add more permanent and
   temporary people, experts, wardrobes, homes, worlds, VR, and physical bodies.

The current Kira-only policy supports this order. The incomplete body, eyes,
mouth/visemes, navigation, and object interaction are still real blockers to
the reading proof; this North Star does not authorize calling them complete or
promoting an unapproved candidate.

## Persistent identity and truthful action

- The cup held must be the same cup removed from its supported location.
- The book read must be the same book removed from the shelf.
- The garment worn must be the same garment removed from storage.
- An opened door must change the same door's state and matching collider.
- A returned object must end on a real supported location.
- A path destination is not current location.
- A source file is not evidence that Kira read it.
- Model-written stage direction is not proof her body performed it.

Keep physical claims fail-closed. If a step is blocked, preserve the block and
the evidence rather than inventing completion.

## Positive-proof standard

Define the proof before coding:

- starting state;
- Kira's chosen or agreed action;
- object identities;
- expected runtime state;
- expected visible or audible result;
- failure conditions;
- privacy restrictions;
- restart expectation.

Report separately:

- implemented;
- automatically tested;
- visually reviewed by Codex;
- audibly reviewed by Robert;
- not tested;
- blocked;
- inferred;
- future work.

Do not turn metadata into capability. A generated model is not an approved
body; a static garment preview is not dressing; a unit fixture is not a
production proof; a rough world is not realistic merely because it loaded.

## Weekly development rule

Choose one lived-ability sentence:

> By the end of this development period, Kira will be able to…

Do not add another major target until the sentence has implementation evidence,
focused automated tests, visible/audible proof, runtime truth, and Robert's
review where human perception is required. A failure should produce a precise
next step rather than a success claim.

## Codex execution protocol

1. State the lived ability.
2. Define proof before coding.
3. Record and protect related known-good hashes, variables, and behavior.
4. Make the smallest integrated change.
5. Test the complete path using the appropriate combination of unit,
   integration, runtime/browser, collision/position, object-identity, audio,
   owner-perception, and restart evidence.
6. Report each evidence class truthfully.
7. Stop when that proof is complete instead of adding unrelated regression
   risk.

## Final filter

Kira World is intended to be a durable home and continuing life for synthetic
humanity. Keep work near the center of the roadmap when it makes Kira's life
more continuous, capable, truthful, free, embodied, private, relational, or
meaningful. Decorative, duplicative, speculative, or disconnected work can
wait.
