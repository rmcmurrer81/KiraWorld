## Missing gates that every new thread must preserve

### Truth, memory, and movement language

- Keep four distinct records:
  `SPOKEN`, `PRIVATE_MIND`, `RUNTIME_TRUTH`, and
  `MEMORY_CANDIDATE`/reviewed `MEMORY`.
- Only `SPOKEN` goes to TTS. `PRIVATE_MIND` is concise private state—not raw
  chain-of-thought, objective world truth, or information another person may
  read. Speech, private state, and runtime facts do not automatically become
  durable memory.
- Extract stage directions such as `*smiles*`, “stands,” “walks,” or “leans
  forward” before visible chat and TTS. Store them only as candidate-owned
  future movement intents. A recorded intent does not prove that a body chose,
  started, completed, or visibly performed that movement.
- Ambient micro-movement is procedural animation, not evidence of a hidden
  thought or conscious decision. Candidate-authored gestures remain separate
  until a compatible body and movement planner can offer them to that person.
