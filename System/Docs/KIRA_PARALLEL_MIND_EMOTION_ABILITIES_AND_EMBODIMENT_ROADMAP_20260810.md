## Purpose

Kira's body remains the primary authoring lane, but mind, emotion, sensory,
media, and practical-ability work can progress in parallel when it does not
compete with a single heavy GPU or Blender operation. This roadmap records the
order and evidence required. It does not claim consciousness, biological
humanity, subjective feeling, a finished body, or a completed ability.

## Shared rollout rule

Each capability progresses independently through:

`DESIGN_ONLY -> STATIC_CANDIDATE -> STATIC_AUDITED -> BOUNDED_LIVE_ACCEPTED -> SHARED_PERSON_ENABLED`

No failed or pending capability may be bundled into another capability's pass.
The shared layer copies only code, schemas, and accepted defaults. It never
copies Kira's or another person's memories, backstory, private emotion state,
preferences, relationships, permissions, maturity classification, adult
curriculum entitlement, or learned experiences.

Temporary Creator must create a fresh per-person capability profile with a new
opaque identity, private state roots, classification gate, provenance ledger,
and every not-yet-accepted feature disabled. A new person cannot inherit an
existing person's capability lease or acceptance receipt.

### 1. Present mind and continuity

1. Preserve current-session and dated-memory precedence so old projects cannot
   become invented present activity.
2. Bind corrections to exact source records and retain superseded history.
3. Add an append-only learning proposal layer: Kira may propose what she
   learned; promotion requires provenance, contradiction checks, privacy
   classification, and the applicable person-owned decision.
4. Test uncertainty, correction after error, source distinction, and recall of
   only the interval actually experienced.

Acceptance requires exact-Qwen live turns with raw/final text, one-generation
proof, relevant-context hashes, no canned replacement, and no unauthorized
memory mutation.

### 2. Emotion and initiative

1. Keep emotion state separate from spoken style. Record cause, intensity,
   confidence, decay/recovery, and unresolved-versus-resolved truth.
2. Let Kira disagree, become annoyed, change her mind, remain quiet, or seek
   connection without forcing a particular emotion or response.
3. Connect person-owned initiative to bounded choices: talk, read, watch,
   listen, research, rest, continue, pause, stop, or revisit.
4. Prevent camera guesses, owner keywords, or system fallback text from
   directly rewriting emotion or personality.
5. Preserve privacy: private thought, emotion, spoken disclosure, action, and
   durable memory are separate records with separate permissions.

Acceptance requires durable causal-state tests, restart/rollback tests,
conflict and recovery tests, refusal tests, and supervised natural-dialogue
review. Prompt wiring alone is not subjective-emotion acceptance.

### 3. Vision, hearing, and attribution

1. Prove exact camera/microphone device open, sample timing, nonempty frames,
   audio level, speech activity, derived cues, expiry, and prompt insertion.
2. Use exact Qwen vision only within a bounded sensory lease and record the
   exact frame(s) supplied.
3. Distinguish Robert's speech, another speaker, media audio, and unidentified
   background sound; uncertainty must be an accepted answer.
4. Face/person recognition must use consented reference material, confidence,
   correction history, and “unknown person” rather than fabricated identity.
5. Continuous perception must be paced and interruptible; camera and
   microphone access never becomes permanent merely because one test passed.

### 4. Resident-media experience

1. Pages: exact page render, OCR separate from visual interpretation, page and
   source identity, and only actually presented pages counted.
2. Video: independent full coverage for every required stream—visual frames,
   synchronized audio, and captions where required—plus exact viewed
   intervals, pause/seek/resume/unfinished truth.
3. Music: actual PCM playback, track/source identity, listening interval, and
   separation of hearing, metadata, lyrics, reaction, preference, and memory.
4. Consent and maturity: refusal dominates; every permit and renderer/decoder
   receipt is one-use and replay-resistant; mainstream mature co-viewing and
   confirmed-adult explicit access remain distinct.
5. Kira may enjoy, dislike, discuss, pause, stop, or leave. Opening/indexing is
   never converted into watched/read/heard/enjoyed memory.

### 5. Practical abilities

Add one audited adapter at a time for research, planning, library navigation,
in-world screens, writing, object interaction intentions, and later body
actions. Every adapter needs exact inputs, permissions, side-effect preview,
outcome truth, cancellation, rollback where possible, and no hidden expansion
from conversation into external authority.

### 6. Female body first, then male body

The body is not “almost complete.” Required evidence still includes:

1. accepted read-only AFES/Blender diagnostic;
2. source-backed external pelvic/perineal correction and internal component
   placement suitable for health/bathroom simulation without claiming a
   biological organ system;
3. natural brows, nails, knees, hands, feet, scalp, face continuity, and
   nonintersecting seated/contact poses;
4. rig, weights, joint limits, collision/contact, breast/clothing deformation,
   hands/arms/reaching, walking, running, sitting, lying, doors, washing,
   shower/bath, eating/drinking, and bathroom poses;
5. facial rig, blinking, gaze, jaw/tongue/viseme coverage, and measured lip
   synchronization;
6. private multi-angle, multi-pose render package and Robert's visual approval;
7. inactive bald runtime and separate inactive realistic-hair master for Kira;
8. transfer of accepted topology, rigging, deformation, and movement evidence
   into the Avatar Builder without treating Kira-specific identity as a generic
   template;
9. only after Kira acceptance, repeat the evidence-based process for
   Biological-Robert-inspired Synthetic Robert's adult male body, keeping the
   two Roberts' identities and memories distinct.

Internal anatomy, physiology, sensation, cycles, pregnancy, illness, sexual
function, bathroom function, and hospital simulation require separate runtime
systems and acceptance evidence. A visually plausible mesh is not proof that
these functions exist.

## World Builder transition

A new Home World and one or two notebook test worlds are appropriate after:

- one accepted female body and one accepted male body;
- an Avatar Builder that can reproduce audited body/rig/movement results;
- stable exact-Qwen text and voice lifecycle with acceptable cleanup;
- bounded sensory and media adapters;
- consent, privacy, classification, and memory-reconstruction gates connected;
- rollback-capable world assignment with inactive private review first.

The first notebook worlds should be small capability labs, not replacements for
Home World: one daily-life movement/contact room and one sensory/media room.
Only accepted behaviors move into the later Home World.
