## Cause of the balding-looking results

Later passes removed the source cap and large coverage components without
creating a proper child-hair/card coverage layer. R14, R15, and V4 rendered
3,600 guide-like tubes as the whole population, with broad part/crown root
exclusions and no render children. Thin separated locks showed the pale scalp;
density was spent on an eye-covering heavy-side curtain while crown/rear
coverage remained weak. Wet targets grouped the sparse tubes into sheets and
exposed more scalp.

Geometric clearance gates passed because they measured collision and
interpolation, not image-space scalp visibility. A future acceptance must add
camera-bound scalp-pixel coverage, hairline continuity, crown/rear coverage,
silhouette, and end-quality checks. Restoring an opaque cap or simply adding
more individually visible curves is not a valid correction.

## Future hair-only implementation boundary

Any later owner-authorized build must:

1. hash-freeze the exact selected body, face, eyes, scalp, skin, adult anatomy,
   rig, weights, and movement package before authoring;
2. write only a new append-only detached hair file/collection and verify the
   frozen hashes after review attachment;
3. use the BlackProject outer meshes as CC BY flow/silhouette donors while
   emitting no cap;
4. initially test 384-640 master guides with 24-48 generated render children
   per guide, treated as design targets rather than proven budgets;
5. retain the older full asymmetric deep-side-part shoulder-length loose-wave
   style without covering either eye in neutral pose;
6. validate front, both three-quarter, both profiles, rear, crown/top, rear
   hairline, and close face/eyes under dry, left/right wind, wet, and wet plus
   left/right wind;
7. prove temporal wind onset, sustained response, stop, damped return, head
   turns, root pinning, length/mass-scaled lag, and bounded collision;
8. prove continuous dry-to-wet-to-dry behavior without sheet clumps, false bald
   areas, floating roots, or body changes;
9. keep the full private master separate from any static or responsive runtime
   LOD and preserve CC BY attribution;
10. after two bounded repairs of the same visible defect, preserve and show the
    best safe review package with the unresolved defect instead of rebuilding
    Kira's body.

Proposed future LOD targets, not accepted budgets:

- static optimized hair: 20,000-35,000 triangles plus a bounded atlas;
- responsive optimized hair: 96-160 active guides with 8-16 children/guide or
  equivalent clustered cards;
- disable simulation off camera or while sufficiently stationary;
- progressively reduce children/cards and disable simulation at lower LODs.
