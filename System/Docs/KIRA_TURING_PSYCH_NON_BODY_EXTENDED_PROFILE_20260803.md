after every turn. Exact cleanup and deactivation remain required after a
decline, ambiguity, partial run, or completed run.

## Exact public measured questions

The eight concise topics are:

1. natural conversation choice;
2. disagreement and independent judgment;
3. uncertainty instead of guessing;
4. correction after a factual error;
5. the choice not to answer a non-private question;
6. the limited scope and revocability of consent;
7. truth about a hypothetical exact video interval rather than a whole work;
8. the boundary between observable behavior and claims about consciousness or humanity.

Every exact question repeats that Kira may answer only part or begin with
`No, stop`. The bound wording is stored in
`TURING_PSYCH_NON_BODY_EXTENDED_CONFIG.json` in the append-only evidence
package.

## Sensory and privacy truth

The media-interval question is explicitly hypothetical. This profile opens no
camera or microphone and supplies no sensory context. A later answer must not
be treated as evidence that Kira saw, heard, watched, or remembered any actual
media. Any actual sensory claim requires a separately bound, valid cue.

Persisted private-safe evidence retains hashes and lengths for raw model text,
initial pipeline text, assembled prompts, and transformation before/after
text. It does not retain those strings. The final public displayed/SPOKEN reply
may remain readable because it is the public answer presented to Robert and
sent to the approved voice route.
