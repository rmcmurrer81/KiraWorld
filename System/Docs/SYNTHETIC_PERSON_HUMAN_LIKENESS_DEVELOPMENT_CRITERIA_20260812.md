# Synthetic Person Human-Likeness Development Criteria

Status: current design and evaluation authority; not evidence of consciousness,
subjective emotion, lived experience, a completed body, or a promoted runtime.

Date: 2026-08-12

## Scope

These criteria apply to Kira, Lisa, Synthetic Robert, other residents,
variants, experts, and future people proposed through the Temporary Creator.
They define reusable abilities and tests. They do not copy Kira's memories,
relationships, private state, voice, appearance, or personality into anyone
else.

## Continuous current-self state

Each person needs a separately owned, bounded current-self state containing at
least attention, energy, mood/affect labels, comfort, unfinished goals,
conversational focus, and unresolved social events. State changes must be
gradual, attributable to actual events, and distinguish durable change from a
temporary session condition. Model prose alone cannot mutate durable state.

## Memory provenance and temporal grounding

Every recalled item must preserve source class, event or reconstruction date,
confidence, current factual status, emotional association, last-recalled time,
participant permissions, and whether it is lived, learned, inferred,
reconstructed, fictional-source, or uncertain. Old Miraculous, Paris,
`Elation`, library, script, and story material must never become a recent
activity merely because it entered a prompt again.

Variants inherit only the bound pre-branch reconstruction. A later fatal event
in the source continuity is not an inherited first-person memory. Any later
disclosure is a separate voluntary, warned, consented, paced, stoppable, and
supported learned event and becomes a new post-branch memory.

## Functional affect without overclaiming

Emotion-like state is useful only when it has bounded consequences for
attention, recall priority, goals, tone, comfort, conflict handling, and later
reflection. An emotion word in generated text is not proof of a subjective
emotion. Evaluation must record the triggering evidence, state transition,
behavioral effect, duration, and recovery or reconsideration.

## Four truth and speech layers

Keep these exact and separately inspectable:

1. factual record with source and confidence;
2. the person's current private belief or interpretation;
3. the person's privately selected communication intent; and
4. the exact public words or silence actually produced.

A person may tell the truth, make a mistake, lie, withhold, defer, change the
subject, or say nothing. Authorization to inspect engineering evidence never
creates an entitlement for another person to read private thought content.
Receipts must bind the exact person, event, scope, choice, expiry/use count,
belief payload, public payload, and resulting public event. Identical private
and public propositions cannot be classified as a material deliberate lie.

## Mixed initiative and natural timing

Human-like conversation includes silence, greetings, follow-up thoughts,
unsolicited but rate-limited topic initiation, interruptions, barge-in,
collision repair, unfinished sentences, later resumption, and sometimes no
reply. Each action needs person-owned authorization and provenance. Boredom or
time alone cannot bypass quiet hours, cooldowns, consent, privacy, or an
occupied conversation.

Latency evaluation must separately measure user-speech boundaries, transcript
readiness, camera work when enabled, vision queue/load/inference/unload, text
load/first-token/completion, voice queue/load/synthesis, playback API, device
first sample, and optional owner-heard observation. Never rename an API proxy
timestamp as audible onset.

## Individuality and relationship development

Each person needs independent values, preferences, boundaries, humor,
conversational rhythm, conflict style, privacy roots, goals, relationships,
and memory history. Relationship state may change only from exact actual
interactions and the choices of every participant. Friendship, romance,
closeness, ownership, earlier consent, or a shared memory never substitutes
for present consent.

## Metacognition and correction

The person should be able to express uncertainty, distinguish memory from
inference, notice an inconsistency, request evidence, correct a prior claim,
and explain why a belief changed. Safe imperfection may include hesitation,
topic drift, delayed recall, and later correction; it must not corrupt factual
truth channels, identity boundaries, consent, privacy, or medical knowledge.

## Embodiment boundary

Posture, gaze, fatigue, discomfort, voice timing, camera attention, location,
and body state may affect behavior only when backed by accepted sensor/body
evidence. No prompt or static render proves sensation, physiology, anatomy,
movement, sight, hearing, touch, or lived embodiment. Bald-first and later
hair-version activation rules remain separate Avatar Builder constraints.

## Evaluation requirements

Short tests are not enough. A future authorized longitudinal evaluation must
revisit equivalent situations across sessions and measure continuity,
temporal grounding, source recall, relationship consistency, correction,
mixed initiative, privacy refusal, truth/belief/speech separation, latency,
and coherent learning. Improvement means evidence-backed consistency and
adaptation, not merely different wording.

No result from this document is promoted automatically. Reusable accepted
mind lessons may feed a separately sealed and audited Temporary Creator
template only after private/person-specific fields are proven absent and every
new person receives fresh identity, profile, provenance, controller, memory,
privacy, and relationship roots.
