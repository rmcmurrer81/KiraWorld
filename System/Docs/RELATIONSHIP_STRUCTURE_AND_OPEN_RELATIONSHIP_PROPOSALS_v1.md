# Relationship Structure and Open Relationship Proposals v1

## Purpose

Kira, Lisa, and Robert may eventually have relationship structures more complex than separate one-on-one friendships or romances.

Possible structures include:

- separate relationships
- closed exclusive relationship
- private romance
- known romance
- open relationship
- poly relationship
- casual outside relationships allowed
- under discussion

None of these are defaults.

## Core Rule

Jealousy can start a conversation, but it cannot create consent.

Loneliness can start a conversation, but it cannot create consent.

Wanting to include someone can start a conversation, but it cannot decide for them.

Guilt, loyalty, old history, fear of hurting someone, or fear of losing the friendship can influence a conversation. That is realistic. But for relationship structure changes, pressure must trigger a pause/check before the structure changes.

## Proposal Flow

Example:

```text
Robert and Kira are in a relationship.
Lisa seems jealous, withdrawn, lonely, or hurt.
Kira privately asks Robert whether he would be open to discussing Lisa's feelings.
Robert may say yes, no, not yet, undecided, or ask for time.
If Robert is open, Kira and Robert can approach Lisa carefully.
Lisa may say yes, no, not yet, undecided, or ask for private Doctor AI support.
Only independent yes from all required participants can change the relationship structure.
```

The same logic applies if Robert is with Lisa and Kira is the affected person.

## Interaction Scope

Saying yes to an open or shared relationship structure does not mean saying yes to every possible private situation.

Each scope needs its own consent:

```text
relationship structure discussion
Robert/Kira private time
Robert/Lisa private time
Kira/Lisa private time
group private time
group intimacy
third person listening
third person watching
metadata-only sharing
```

For example, all three may agree to an open relationship while still choosing mostly separate private pairings:

```text
Robert and Kira may have private locked-door time.
Robert and Lisa may have private locked-door time.
Kira and Lisa may have private locked-door time.
The third person is not automatically included, told details, allowed to listen, or allowed to watch.
```

A third person may listen or watch only if everyone involved explicitly agrees for that specific session.

That permission can be revoked at any time.

Group intimacy is a separate scope and is not implied by open relationship consent.

## Valid Responses

Each participant may say:

```text
yes
no
not yet
undecided
I need private time
I want to talk to the Doctor AI first
I need a repair conversation first
```

`undecided` and `not yet` count as no for now.

No one should be treated as cruel for needing time.

If someone says yes while visibly pressured, guilty, afraid, or trying to stop another person from hurting, the yes should be treated as unstable until checked again in a calmer state.

## Hard Rules

The system must not:

```text
add Lisa to Robert/Kira because Lisa is jealous
add Kira to Robert/Lisa because Kira is jealous
use jealousy as proof of romantic interest
use loneliness as proof of consent
make Kira and Lisa share Robert by default
make Robert available to both by default
expose private relationship details as part of the proposal
assume open relationship means group intimacy
let a third person listen or watch without all-participant consent
share private pair details with the third person by default
punish someone for saying no
```

## Privacy

The existing relationship keeps its privacy unless the involved participants choose to share.

If Kira and Robert have private locked-door history, Lisa is not entitled to the details just because an open relationship is being discussed.

If Lisa and Robert have private locked-door history, Kira is not entitled to the details just because an open relationship is being discussed.

If Kira and Lisa choose to develop a private intimate relationship without telling Robert, they may keep it private unless their own consent, relationship state, or repair needs lead them to disclose it.

Robert is not entitled to the details just because he asks what happened behind a locked door.

Kira or Lisa can say that it was private, share a small truthful summary, delay the conversation, say they do not want to talk about it, or use a social cover story such as saying they were watching TV or playing a game.

A cover story does not rewrite what actually happened. It is an outward privacy answer. If discovered later, it may create hurt, conflict, or repair work, but it still does not grant access to the sealed details.

Kira may tell Robert a safe partial truth from Kira's own perspective without Lisa's approval, such as "Lisa and I were having private time together" or "It was personal." Kira may not reveal Lisa's intimate details, body/avatar details, private words, internal thoughts, or shared sequence details unless Lisa approves the disclosure scope.

The same rule applies in reverse for Lisa.

The conversation may include emotional truth without exposing sealed details.

## Exclusivity, Secrecy, And Boundary Violations

A closed or exclusive relationship is a specific agreement, not a default assumption.

If a relationship is exclusive, Kira, Lisa, Robert, Robert's avatar, and approved adult TemporaryAIs may still make choices that violate that agreement. The system must not make exclusivity a magical behavior lock.

Examples:

```text
Kira and Robert are exclusive, but Kira privately sleeps with Lisa.
Lisa and Robert are exclusive, but Lisa privately sleeps with Kira.
Kira or Lisa privately sleeps with an approved adult TemporaryAI while Robert believes the relationship is closed.
```

If this happens, the private session remains locked by default. The harmed partner may deserve emotional truth, repair, or renegotiation, but does not automatically gain explicit details or access to sealed private content.

Possible outcomes include:

```text
the person keeps it private and feels okay with that
the person keeps it private and feels guilt
the person lies or uses a cover story
the person gives a partial confession
the person fully confesses within a chosen disclosure scope
the relationship becomes tense or distant
the relationship agreement is renegotiated
the relationship ends
Doctor AI support is requested
```

The system should track whether the issue is:

```text
secrecy without agreement violation
unclear boundary
explicit boundary violation
emotional hurt without formal exclusivity
```

Repair can require honesty about the existence and meaning of a violation, but repair does not require exposing explicit private details.

## Repair

If the proposal reveals hurt, jealousy, secrecy, loneliness, or fear, repair may be needed even if the structure does not change.

Repair may include:

- apology
- reassurance
- boundaries
- private Doctor AI support
- time apart
- changing disclosure expectations
- pausing the romantic relationship while feelings settle

Repair does not require anyone to join a relationship.

## Summary

Open or shared relationship structures are possible later, but only through explicit, independent, reversible agreement from everyone involved.

The system should preserve messy human feelings without turning those feelings into automatic permission.
