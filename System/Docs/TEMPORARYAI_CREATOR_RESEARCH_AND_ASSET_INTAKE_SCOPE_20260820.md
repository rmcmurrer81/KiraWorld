# TemporaryAI Creator research and asset-intake scope

Status: owner design intent with partial source/tools/manifests present; not a
claim that every intake, licensing, generation, or activation stage is complete.

TemporaryAI Creator is intended to do more than produce a name and a short
profile. For each approved candidate or expert it should assemble a reviewable
creation workspace containing:

- reliable research used to build the candidate's bounded mind, knowledge,
  backstory, behavior, and uncertainty boundaries;
- picture and video references useful to Avatar Builder and body construction;
- voice-reference candidates useful to Voice Creator;
- source URLs, capture dates, hashes, speaker/subject confidence, rights and
  consent status, and accepted/rejected review decisions; and
- the resulting candidate profile, avatar/body requests, voice-discovery
  request, and activation blockers.

The gatherer may search and organize material, but gathering is not itself a
license, consent receipt, or activation approval. Raw source media should remain
in a restricted local/reference lane unless its transfer rights are documented.
Generated or transformed output must remain labeled with its real status and
must not impersonate a real performer or imply studio endorsement.

Robert's Peter Parker design uses a performance direction informed by Tom
Holland's portrayal, which explains the intended resemblance of the existing
private synthetic output. That design intent does not establish Tom Holland's
consent, an exact-voice-cloning right, endorsement, or public redistribution
permission. The same rule applies to the existing Marinette/Ladybug and Kathryn
reference directions.

Current source includes research queues, source packs, avatar-reference intake,
voice-discovery requests, and generated private review artifacts. A future
release claim requires an end-to-end test proving that TemporaryAI Creator can
gather, deduplicate, label, hash, rights-screen, route, and preserve review
decisions without silently activating a voice, body, or public identity.
