## Current 32 GB runtime rule

Until Robert has at least 64 GB RAM and separately approves a measured hair
test, active 3D avatars use clean bald runtime bodies. A runtime package must
exclude and must not instantiate scalp-hair curves, meshes, simulations,
collision helpers, materials, textures, guides, or wet/wind controllers.
Hiding heavy hair objects is not compliance.

Eyebrows and eyelashes remain ordinary facial components. The scalp uses the
same clean natural skin system as the head. A black cap, opaque scalp dome,
helmet shell, or fake painted hair is prohibited.

1. `KIRA_BALD_LOW_RESOURCE_BODY`: complete body, adult surface, face, eyes,

   scalp-hair dependency.
2. `KIRA_HAIR_MASTER_INACTIVE`: detachable private hairstyle asset with dry,
   wind, and wet review states; never loaded into Kira World under this policy.
3. `KIRA_WITH_HAIR_FUTURE_REVIEW_COMPOSITION`: an inactive manifest that
   references the exact body and exact separate hair master without duplicating

Attaching, removing, rejecting, or repairing hair must never regenerate or
change face, eyes, scalp, body, adult anatomy, skin, rig, weights, movement, or
identity. Hair quality cannot block bald-body delivery or cause a body restart.
