# Private Adult Library Policy v1

Robert's private adult media is split by medium:

```text
Data/library/private_adult_text/
Data/library/private_adult_videos/
```

## Organization

- `private_adult_text` is for adult books, magazines, PDFs, documents, and text-like sources.
- `private_adult_videos` is for adult video files.
- Do not store books or magazines under the video shelf.

## Kira/Lisa Access

Adult material should not appear in ordinary school sessions, ordinary classes, or general young/education-oriented reading.

It may be considered for a mature private life-loop context only if:

- the AI is adult-coded,
- the session is explicitly adult/private,
- Robert intentionally enables that library scope,
- the material is treated as source/media, not lived memory,
- consent/privacy boundaries are active,
- and the output is reviewed with care.

This policy does not automatically grant access. It prevents accidental access while preserving the possibility of adult private exploration later.

## OCR

Adult text should not be auto-OCRed by default. If OCR is needed later, it should be requested explicitly as a private/adult repair task.
