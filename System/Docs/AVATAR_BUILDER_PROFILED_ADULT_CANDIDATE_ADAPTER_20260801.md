## Fixed build order

The builder performs this order and records it in `BUILD_EVIDENCE.json`:

1. Parse only the official MakeHuman `body` face group.
2. Apply the two exact female macro targets.
3. Apply the validator-resolved 12 style targets in profile order.
4. Uniformly scale the resulting source to exactly 1.651 m.
5. Attach the official skeleton and per-vertex top-four normalized weights,
   with a root fallback for any source vertex lacking a valid assignment.
6. Run bounded exact source self-intersection cleanup.
7. Run `generic_continuous_adult_female_external_surface_v1` with its authoring
   frame, displacement distance, and degeneracy-area threshold scaled from the
   1.700 m baseline to 1.651 m.
8. Refine that exact continuous v1 result in place with the versioned
   `generic_continuous_adult_female_external_surface_v2` structured field. The
   v2 pass preserves topology, normalized rig weights, and landmark names,
   uses a curved posterior frame, and must add zero exact intersections.
9. Retain every `AFES_LANDMARK__*` group emitted or rebound by the continuous
   adult surface methods.
10. Add official helper-eye geometry at the R13 fit scale (0.855) and 2.2 mm
   posterior inset, plus natural brown iris, limbal, pupil, and transparent
   cornea components. No dedicated black eye-band object is authored; absence
   of a visual dark-band artifact remains pending rendered review.
11. Add 10 natural fingernails and 10 natural toenails from official terminal
    rig-bone landmarks. Their surface fit remains pending measured/visual
    review rather than being inferred from placement heuristics.
12. Solve each knee skeleton chain independently from measured posterior +Y
    ankle movement and create bilateral quaternion actions. This proves bone
    kinematics only, not the skinned knee mesh.
13. Under the active 32 GB delivery policy, instantiate no scalp-hair provider
    or asset. A future hair-bearing composition is a separate inactive lane
    and never changes or regenerates this body.

There is no import of a prior Kira body builder, Robert body builder, rejected

## Appearance bindings

- Skin: warm `#C7A08E` sRGB, converted with the standard piecewise sRGB-to-linear
  transfer function. A bounded 3.5% procedural Noise/Color-Ramp variation is
  used instead of a flat or pale material. The R15 repair explicitly sets the
  Principled subsurface scale to `0.00125 m`; Blender's wax-like `0.05 m`
  default is forbidden. A subtle bounded micro-bump is also recorded.
- Eyes: natural brown profile, measured official-helper socket fit, one
  continuous non-overlapping pupil/iris/limbal disc per eye, procedural radial
  fibre detail, and a separately spaced transparent cornea cap. The component
  gate measures body clearance, requires zero body/eye triangle overlaps, and
  refuses coplanar pupil/iris faces. Rendered black-band and appearance review
  remains independently required.
- Nails: all 20 expected components are ray-projected as small conformal plates
  from their exact terminal rig bones. Each grid point is fitted to the body
  surface, toe orientation comes from the projected surface frame, clearance
  is bounded to `0.000025-0.00040 m`, and body/nail triangle overlap must be
  zero. Visual appearance review remains required even after those geometric
  gates pass.
- Knees: the body Armature modifier uses preserve-volume deformation. The
  solver targets a conservative review bend instead of maximum ankle travel
  and evaluates the real deformed body at 30, 55, and 80 degrees. Lateral
  width, face-area, and edge-length percentile bounds must all pass before the
  builder can continue. Dedicated bilateral renders and independent visual
  review remain required.
- Hair: the current complete delivery lane is bald and must prove zero
  scalp-hair providers, objects, materials, textures, node groups, controllers,
  or hidden dependencies. The legacy responsive-provider contract and its
  evidence remain historical research only. A future detachable master must
  live in a separately versioned private lane, pass dry/wind/wet visual review,
  and remain disabled until the 64 GB and explicit owner performance-test
  gates pass.

## Invocation contract

The builder must run in a dedicated background process with factory startup.
It refuses interactive Blender and refuses to clear any loaded `.blend`:

```text
blender --background --factory-startup \
  --python tools/blender_build_profiled_kira_adult_candidate.py --
```

The required Blender-side arguments after `--` are:

```text
--output-dir Avatar/private_owner_review/kira_profiled_adult_candidate_<new_id>
--acknowledge-inactive-private-candidate
```

Optional, separately authorized outputs:

```text
--render-owner-review
--export-private-glb
```
