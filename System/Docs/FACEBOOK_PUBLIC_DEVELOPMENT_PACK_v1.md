## Required truth labels

- `CURRENT PROTOTYPE`: present software that may still be incomplete or
  unreliable.
- `ENGINEERING TEST`: a bounded technical check; not proof of natural
  appearance or complete daily-life behavior.
- `CONCEPT ART`: an illustration of intended direction; not gameplay.
- `PLANNED`: not yet built and verified.
- `LONG-TERM GOAL`: a direction without a promised date.
