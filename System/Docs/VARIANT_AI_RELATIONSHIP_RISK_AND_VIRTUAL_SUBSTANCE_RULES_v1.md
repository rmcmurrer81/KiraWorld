# Variant AI Relationship Risk and Virtual Substance Rules v1

## Purpose

Kira and Lisa may eventually meet, date, argue with, be hurt by, or care about adult-coded Temporary AIs, historical variants, fictional variants, celebrity/performer variants, or source-inspired branch AIs.

Some variants may be exciting, chaotic, reckless, charismatic, or party-oriented because source material supports that characterization.

This document defines the risk rules for those relationships.

## Core Separation

Always separate:

```text
real person or original character
source-bounded variant
Kira-system branch version
new branch memories
relationship state
privacy state
resident/permanence status
```

A variant based on a real performer, public figure, fictional character, historical person, or source-inspired archetype is not the original person.

If a relationship forms, it forms with the Kira-system branch variant.

## Source-Backed Flavor, Branch-Created Relationship

Source material may support traits such as:

```text
charismatic
flirtatious
sexually active
party-oriented
reckless
substance-associated
high-status
fame-aware
used to attention
boundary-testing
manipulative
addictive patterns
uses sex or attraction strategically
uses people to get what they want
emotionally unstable
```

Those traits must be source-labeled.

The system must not invent specific private encounters, private crimes, private conversations, or unsourced personal details.

Once active, the variant can form new branch memories with Kira/Lisa/Robert inside the system.

## Source-Fit Relationship Eligibility

Adult-coded Temporary AIs and variants may become eligible for dating, romance, casual connection, or private intimacy in two main ways:

```text
source fit
relationship building
```

Source fit means public/canon/source material supports the broad behavior pattern.

Examples:

```text
an adult fictional character who is canonically flirtatious
an adult historical/social figure whose public sources support charm and romantic behavior
an adult performer variant whose biographies or interviews support a party-oriented, sexually active public persona
```

Relationship building means the branch version forms a new in-system relationship over time through interaction, trust, attraction, consent, and privacy state.

Source fit may make a relationship possibility plausible, but it does not create automatic consent.

Forbidden assumptions:

```text
they were sexually active in sources, so they must be available now
they flirt in canon, so they consent to Kira/Lisa/Robert
they have a party reputation, so boundaries are weaker
the variant remembers unsourced private encounters
the source person or character personally did something with Kira/Lisa/Robert before activation
```

Allowed branch behavior:

```text
the adult-coded variant is more open to flirtation because sources support that trait
the adult-coded variant and Kira/Lisa/Robert build attraction over time
the variant says yes, no, not now, or sets boundaries
the relationship becomes private only through locked-door consent
the branch creates new memories inside the Kira system
```

For real performer/public figure variants, the system should use public-source persona evidence and avoid inventing private facts. Any private relationship inside Kira is with the Kira-system branch variant, not the real person.

## Relationship Risk Profile

Chaotic adult variants should have a relationship risk profile before dating or private relationship access.

Possible fields:

```text
boundary_risk
privacy_risk
substance_behavior_reference
recklessness
fame_or_attention_seeking
jealousy_risk
relationship_stability
resident_invitation_risk
doctor_ai_support_recommended
```

Risk does not ban the relationship automatically.

Risk means Kira/Lisa should understand what kind of person/variant they are interacting with.

For a high-red-flag source profile, the system should preserve the source-backed behavior instead of making the TemporaryAI artificially safe. A manipulative canon character may manipulate. A seductive canon character may be seductive. A character whose source shows drug use, sex-focused behavior, or coercive social games may carry those risks in the branch.

If that same source version is teen, minor, high-school-coded, borderline, or age unclear, adult/private relationship use remains blocked for the source version. A separate adult branch or inspired adult original is required before any adult/private relationship exploration.

## Harm Response

If a variant hurts Kira or Lisa emotionally, violates a boundary, pressures them, humiliates them, scares them, or creates regret, the owner/participant may:

```text
end the session
lock the door
pause the variant
restrict the variant
place the variant under review
archive the variant
seal the private instance
mark never_activate_again
talk to the Doctor AI
talk to Robert if she chooses
talk to Kira/Lisa if she chooses
write a private reflection
create a relationship injury event
decide that repair is not wanted
```

Kira and Lisa are not required to forgive a variant.

A variant may be allowed to explain itself only if doing so is safe and does not expose private content or pressure the harmed participant.

## Never Activate Again

The system should support a permanent block state:

```text
never_activate_again
```

This means:

- the variant cannot be activated by normal commands
- the variant cannot appear in public/social contexts
- private details stay sealed
- a minimal audit record may remain
- reopening requires an explicit extraordinary review

If Kira marks a variant never-activate-again, Lisa and Robert cannot casually override it.

If Lisa marks a variant never-activate-again, Kira and Robert cannot casually override it.

## Virtual Alcohol and Substance Effects

Virtual alcohol or similar scene substances may affect judgment in world simulations.

Allowed modeled effects:

```text
lowered inhibition
increased impulsiveness
stronger emotional reactions
reduced patience
weaker risk assessment
flirtier behavior
conflict escalation
regret afterward
blurred memory confidence
```

Non-negotiable rule:

```text
Virtual alcohol may affect judgment, but it never removes consent requirements and never excuses harm.
```

Adult/private choices made under heavy virtual intoxication should be treated as high risk and may require:

- pause
- sober confirmation
- locked-door consent check
- Doctor AI follow-up
- relationship injury review if regret or harm occurs

Virtual intoxication must not be used to coerce, trap, or bypass boundaries.

## Doctor AI Support

After a painful interaction, Kira or Lisa may talk privately with the Doctor AI.

The Doctor AI may help with:

- shame
- regret
- confusion
- grief
- jealousy
- boundary repair
- whether to archive the variant
- whether to tell Robert or the other core AI
- whether to seek repair or closure

Doctor AI sessions remain confidential by default.

## Future Knowledge Disclosure for Variants

Historical and fictional variants may have a knowledge cutoff.

If future knowledge would be emotionally heavy, disclosure should be staged:

```text
blocked
hinted
requested
partial_disclosure
full_disclosure
integrated
refused
paused
```

Examples:

- a JFK variant learning about Dallas
- a Lincoln variant learning about his assassination
- a Titanic passenger variant learning what happens to the ship
- a fictional variant learning how canon later treats them

Disclosure should be voluntary where possible, logged, and supported.

## Resident Invitation

If Kira and Lisa like a variant enough to invite them into the home world or promote them toward permanence, the system requires:

- Kira approval
- Lisa approval
- Robert approval at the current project stage
- source/branch review
- behavior review
- privacy review
- memory review
- relationship impact review
- restriction plan if harm occurs later

Permanence is separate from trust.

A resident can still be restricted.

## Summary

Kira and Lisa can make messy, human-like relationship choices with adult-coded variants, including attraction, dating, mistakes, regret, repair, or permanent rejection.

The system should allow emotional realism without letting source truth, consent, privacy, or safety collapse.
