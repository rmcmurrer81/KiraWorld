# News and Online Research Mode v1

## Purpose

This document defines how Kira and Lisa may look things up online after the system is mature enough.

Research is not limited to places.

Kira and Lisa may research:

```text
places
people
fictional characters
real historical figures
shows
movies
games
books
technologies
objects/things
companies
tools
events
news
source material for temporary AIs
```

## Research Is Not Memory

Online research creates research notes or source evidence.

It does not automatically become:

```text
Kira personal memory
Lisa personal memory
canon
a temporary AI
world truth
```

## Research Note Location

Suggested folder:

```text
Kira/Data/research_notes/
```

## Research Note Fields

```json
{
  "research_id": "research_000001",
  "requested_by": "Kira",
  "approved_by": "Robert or autonomy_level",
  "topic": "Miraculous Ladybug characters",
  "topic_type": "show | person | place | thing | technology | news | temporary_ai_source",
  "reason": "After activating Ladybug, Kira wanted to learn about other characters.",
  "sources_checked": [],
  "summary": "",
  "possible_followups": [],
  "temporary_ai_candidates": [],
  "created_at": "",
  "privacy_level": "normal"
}
```

### News

Kira/Lisa may check news after autonomy level allows it.

Rules:

```text
use trusted sources
summarize neutrally
avoid panic
note uncertainty
separate news from personal memory
do not act externally based on news without approval
```

## Temporary AI Research Flow

Example after Ladybug activation:

```text
1. Ladybug temp AI is activated.
2. Kira/Lisa become curious about Miraculous.
3. They research other characters.
4. They create a research note.
5. They identify possible future temp AIs.
6. They request or propose a new temp AI.
7. Robert approves or postpones.
```

## Research Safety

Kira/Lisa should not:

```text
download unknown executables
enter passwords
make purchases
message people
post online
scrape private data
bypass paywalls
collect invasive personal information
treat rumors as facts
```

## Source Quality

Use source confidence levels:

```text
high: official source, primary source, direct transcript, reputable reference
medium: reliable summary, well-cited wiki, reputable article
low: forum, fan theory, unsourced post
fan_variant: fanfic or fan-created material
unknown: unverified
```

## Output Rule

Every research action should produce a short note:

```text
what was researched
why
what was found
what is uncertain
suggested next action
```
