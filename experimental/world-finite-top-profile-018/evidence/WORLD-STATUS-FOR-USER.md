# Current World Builder status

World Builder has a working installed foundation: the workspace launches,
original procedural bed frames can be created, existing worlds and studies are
preserved, and bedding engineering studies have portable previews. World015 is
still the installed version; the latest physics changes are separate candidates.

Today's isolated physics work made useful progress: the full-cloth command test
ran1110 frames without a reported overlap, and its first30 release frames had
much smaller stretch and speed peaks than the preserved baseline. It still needs
an independent contact check and visual review, and the new bookkeeping is slow.

The product is not yet a complete realistic furnished-world generator. Reliable
cloth/foam/full-body interactions, complete world placement, and end-to-end
validation of one real and one fictional location remain unfinished. No visual,
physics-quality or owner acceptance is claimed. Video Studio remains primary.

Sources: current World handoff and installed015 receipt, together with isolated
017 final result/assessment and018 profile. The profile is measurement only;
nothing new was installed and no speed improvement is claimed.
