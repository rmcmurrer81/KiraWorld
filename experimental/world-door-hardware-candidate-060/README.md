# Door hardware collision correction — isolated candidate060

The installed056 doors have working hinges, but their collision boxes enclose only the leaf and frame. The actual authored handles protrude beyond the leaf. A CPU reproduction found twelve positions across all six synthetic Mars doors where the normal walking footprint intersects a rendered handle while every existing structure, door and furniture check permits occupancy. Both X and Z door orientations are affected. This is a collision issue, not a new visual-quality verdict.

The leaf is 0.065 m thick. Each handle center is 0.0875 m from its center and the cylinder radius is 0.023 m. Mounts extend from 0.031 to 0.096 m. The candidate uses a small combined handle/mount box on each side, spanning 0.031 to 0.1105 m along the normal, 0.075 m along the door and 0.27 m vertically. Separate thin panel boxes also enclose the applied surface panels. The existing leaf mesh, leaf collider, frames, hinge poses and moving speed stay exact. Hardware collision is conservative; it does not simulate contact forces or fingers gripping a handle.

Using a uniformly deeper door slab would also stop walking through empty space away from the handle. The tests demonstrate twelve such false blocks per six-door layout that the separate boxes avoid. All hardware boxes rotate about the same hinge as their actual meshes. Swing reservations expand on the hardware side; the far-jamb radius stays bounded by the leaf, because the handle is mounted at 0.84 leaf-width from the hinge. The initial broader sweep moved plaques, so its result and diff remain in `history/`; that variant is not the delivered candidate. The final candidate leaves every existing dressing placement exact in all tested layouts, including the separate059 meal-station plan.

Five layouts passed: base, renamed IDs, wider, translated, and the actual saved Mars geometry. Both preview and package controller copies were checked. Evidence covers 540 door poses, actual mesh vertices at nine opening positions per door, fixed leaf/frame geometry, forward and reverse open-door passage, closed-door blocking, restored closure, paired-airlock interlocks, and motion pausing when hardware would sweep through the walker. The original twelve allowed penetrations are now blocked by both controllers. A separate 24-door test preserves the existing maximum: 192 door collision parts are checked in batches of at most 128, without increasing the navigation module's bound or dropping parts in later batches. Missing, altered or reordered metadata hardware definitions are rejected. These are CPU assertions, not hundreds of thousands of independent tests or visual approval.

The five changed files are the two walk controllers, shared scene metadata, producer pins and the exporter's preview-controller pin. Portable metadata gains four attached hardware colliders per door, linked to the existing leaf and its hinge through local offsets. It remains descriptive metadata; no engine or VR collision adapter is supplied. The actual saved056 preview still verifies and remains export-eligible through the installed exporter. Candidate060 holds that old preview rather than silently exporting behavior absent from its saved controller. A new exact preview is required before a future060 export. No new preview or GLB was created for this candidate.

All 116 protected owner files, the 42-file canonical closure and selected saved-job pointer were verified unchanged. No model, GPU, browser/native UI, installation or owner-state write occurred.
