What this study proves

Original mattress vertices integrate velocity and compliant constraints. Cloth edge and triangle contacts modify the same simulation vertices that are rendered; there is no visual clipping or hidden replacement surface. The pillow's support follows the mattress height.

This remains an uncalibrated fixed-bottom height field and one-way support model. Contacts are discrete and use vertical upper-surface projection; continuous collision, cloth self-collision, cloth-pillow collision, general underside/closed-volume contact, whole-body interaction and world placement are absent. The exact saved frame uses discrete convex contact with the same chamfered parts that are displayed. Individual slat-pressure response is not simulated. The frame contact count reports remaining triangle/solid intersections; it is not a continuous-collision or visual-quality guarantee. The 220 N force is authored. Hiding the blanket changes visibility only. No finished-bed or photoreal acceptance is claimed.
