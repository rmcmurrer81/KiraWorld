# Sustainable Fashion Research Plan Revision Update

## Introduction

As a fashion designer and advocate for sustainable practices, I am committed to exploring innovative methods for reducing waste and promoting environmentally friendly production processes. This research plan aims to identify key areas of focus and develop strategies for integrating sustainable materials and techniques into my design work.

## Methodology

* Conduct thorough literature review on sustainable fashion production methods
* Analyze existing research on environmental impact of textile industry
* Identify opportunities for innovation in sustainable material sourcing and processing
