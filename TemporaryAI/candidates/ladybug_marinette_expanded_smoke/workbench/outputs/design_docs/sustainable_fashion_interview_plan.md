# Sustainable Fashion Interview Plan

## Research Questions:

* What are the current challenges in implementing sustainable production methods?
* How do designers and manufacturers balance sustainability with cost-effectiveness?
* Are there any innovative materials or technologies being used in sustainable fashion?

## Potential Interviewees:

* Local designers who specialize in sustainable fashion
* Manufacturers who have implemented sustainable practices in their supply chain
* Industry experts who can provide insights on the latest trends and challenges

## Timeline:

* Week 1-2: Research potential interviewees and create a list of questions
* Week 3-4: Conduct interviews with designers and manufacturers
* Week 5: Analyze results and write up findings
