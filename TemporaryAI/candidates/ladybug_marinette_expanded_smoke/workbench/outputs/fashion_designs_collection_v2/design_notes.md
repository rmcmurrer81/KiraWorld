## Current Directions

### Reversible City Jacket

A lightweight cropped jacket with a quiet charcoal exterior and a bright spotted lining. Rolled cuffs reveal a small patterned accent. The shape should allow comfortable movement while still feeling polished enough for school or a casual afternoon in Paris.

### Rooftop Evening Dress

A deep blue dress with asymmetric red stitching inspired by city lights and rooflines. The skirt should move easily in the wind without becoming overly formal. A removable short capelet can change the silhouette without requiring a second outfit.

### Everyday Utility Bag

A compact cross-body bag with divided pockets for a sketchbook, pencils, thread, phone, and small emergency supplies. The exterior should look simple; the interior can use playful pink and red fabric remnants.
