# Sustainable Fashion Research Plan Revision

## Abstract

This revised research plan builds upon previous work in sustainable fashion, focusing on Parisian fashion concepts that incorporate eco-friendly materials and production methods. Our goal is to create a comprehensive guide for designers and manufacturers looking to transition towards more environmentally responsible practices.

## Materials Sourcing

* We will prioritize sourcing materials from local suppliers whenever possible.
* Consider using recycled or upcycled materials in designs.
* Research sustainable fabric options, such as organic cotton, hemp, and Tencel.

## Production Methods

* Implement a take-back program for old garments to encourage recycling and reduce waste.
* Explore the use of 3D printing technology for creating complex designs while minimizing material usage.
* Investigate the potential for repurposing or upcycling existing materials in production processes.

## Supply Chain Management

* Develop partnerships with suppliers who share our commitment to sustainability.
* Implement a system for tracking and monitoring carbon emissions throughout the supply chain.
* Research the feasibility of using renewable energy sources in manufacturing facilities.
