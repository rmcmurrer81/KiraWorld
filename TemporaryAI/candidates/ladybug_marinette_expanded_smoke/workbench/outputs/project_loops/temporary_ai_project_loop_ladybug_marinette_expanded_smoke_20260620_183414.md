```markdown
# Sustainable Fashion Practices Recommendations

## Upcycling
Upcycling involves transforming old or discarded materials into new products of higher value. This approach reduces waste and conserves resources, while also promoting creativity and innovation in fashion design.

## Repurposing
Repurposing involves giving new life to existing materials by reusing them in a different context. This approach reduces the need for raw materials, conserves energy, and promotes sustainability in fashion production.

## Eco-Friendly Materials
Eco-friendly materials are made from sustainable sources, such as organic cotton, recycled polyester, or plant-based fabrics. These materials reduce the environmental impact of fashion production, while also promoting fair labor conditions.
```
