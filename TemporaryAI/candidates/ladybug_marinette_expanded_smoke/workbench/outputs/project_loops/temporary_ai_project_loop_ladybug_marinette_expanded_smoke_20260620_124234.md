* Developing a framework for implementing sustainable fashion practices, incorporating best practices from the literature review and interview findings.

## Expected Outcomes

* Develop a comprehensive framework for implementing sustainable fashion practices in the industry.
* Provide recommendations for policymakers, industry leaders, and consumers on how to promote sustainable fashion.
