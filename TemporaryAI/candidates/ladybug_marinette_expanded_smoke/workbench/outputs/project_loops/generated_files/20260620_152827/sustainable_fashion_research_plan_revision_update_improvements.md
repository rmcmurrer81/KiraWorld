# Sustainable Production Methods

## The Ordinary's Clinical Formulations with Integrity

The Ordinary is a brand that prioritizes sustainability and transparency in their products. Their clinical formulations are designed to be effective while minimizing waste and environmental impact. We can explore how these formulations can inform our sustainable fashion research plan.

### Key Takeaways:

* Reduce, Reuse, Recycle: Implement strategies for reducing waste, reusing materials, and recycling whenever possible.
* Sustainable Ingredients: Choose ingredients that are sustainably sourced and have minimal environmental impact.
* Minimal Packaging: Optimize packaging to minimize waste while maintaining product integrity.
