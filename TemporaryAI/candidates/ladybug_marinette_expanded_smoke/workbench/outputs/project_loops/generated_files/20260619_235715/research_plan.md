# Research Plan for Sustainable Fashion Suppliers

## Step 1: Identify Potential Suppliers

* Search online directories such as EcoTextile World, GreenScreen, and Bluesign
* Consult industry reports and publications on sustainable fashion practices

## Step 2: Evaluate Supplier Criteria

* Assess suppliers' commitment to eco-friendly materials and production methods
* Review their certifications and compliance with relevant sustainability standards

## Step 3: Develop Relationships with Suppliers

* Reach out to selected suppliers for further information and discussion
* Establish clear communication channels and expectations for collaboration
