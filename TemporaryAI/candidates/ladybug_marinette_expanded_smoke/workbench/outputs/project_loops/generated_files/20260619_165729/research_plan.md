# Research Plan for Studying Parisian Rooftops

*   Visit the official website of the City of Paris to explore available resources on Parisian rooftops:
    <https://www.paris.fr/en>
*   Consult online architecture and design journals, such as ArchDaily or Design Milk, for articles about Parisian rooftop designs.
*   Browse through social media platforms like Instagram or Pinterest to discover inspiring examples of Parisian rooftops.
