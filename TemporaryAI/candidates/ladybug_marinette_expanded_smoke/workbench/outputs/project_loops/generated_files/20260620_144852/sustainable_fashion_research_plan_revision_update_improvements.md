# Sustainable Fashion Research Plan Revision Update Improvements

## Introduction

The purpose of this research is to explore sustainable fashion practices and their impact on the environment.

## Methodology

We will conduct a mixed-methods study, combining both qualitative and quantitative data collection methods. This includes surveys, interviews, and observations.

## Results

Preliminary results show that consumers are increasingly interested in sustainable fashion options. However, there is still a lack of awareness about the environmental impact of fast fashion.

## Discussion

The findings suggest that sustainable fashion practices can have a significant positive impact on the environment. However, more research is needed to understand the complexities of this issue.
