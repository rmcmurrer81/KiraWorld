# Sustainable Fashion Research Plan Revision Update Improvements

## Introduction
This research plan aims to explore innovative ways to source sustainable materials for the fashion industry. We will discuss potential suppliers, assess their environmental impact, and identify opportunities for improvement.

## Objectives
1. Identify 5 potential suppliers of sustainable materials.
2. Evaluate the environmental impact of each supplier using a scoring system (e.g., carbon footprint, water usage).
3. Research innovative technologies for material conversion (e.g., upcycling, recycling).

## Methodology

### Step 1: Supplier Identification
We will research and identify 5 potential suppliers of sustainable materials through online directories and industry reports.

### Step 2: Environmental Impact Assessment
Using a scoring system, we will evaluate the environmental impact of each supplier based on their carbon footprint, water usage, and other relevant factors.

### Step 3: Innovative Technologies Research
We will research innovative technologies for material conversion, including upcycling and recycling methods.

## Expected Outcomes

* A list of 5 potential suppliers of sustainable materials.
* An assessment of the environmental impact of each supplier using a scoring system.
* Identification of opportunities for improvement in sourcing sustainable materials.
