# Home World Main House Blueprint v1

Date: 2026-06-30
Status: blueprint-first build basis, revised 2026-07-01 with required fixtures and AI Body Spa

## Intent

Create the first stable Home World / Main World location: a two-story, unfurnished five-bedroom house with a small strip mall across the street. This is the default safe place for Kira, Lisa, Robert's avatar/AI, guests, and temporary residents while other notebook worlds are still under construction.

## Source Basis

This is an original layout, not a copied commercial floor plan. It uses public five-bedroom home-plan examples only for scale sanity:

- Public five-bedroom two-story home examples commonly fall around 3,000-3,600 sq ft.
- A common approximate footprint for this size is about 52 ft x 51 ft 6 in, which maps well to roughly 16.0 m x 15.7 m.
- This build uses a simplified 16.0 m wide x 15.5 m deep footprint so it can be walked at human scale in the Kira shell.

## Site Plan

- House centered on a quiet residential lot.
- Street in front of the house.
- Strip mall directly across the street.
- Sidewalks and road crossing are included so Robert and active AIs can walk between home and expert offices later.
- Minimal required furniture/fixtures are included before decoration: beds, bathroom fixtures, kitchen blockout, and Marinette's temporary computer/notebook workbench.

## House Footprint

- Overall footprint: 16.0 m wide x 15.5 m deep.
- Two stories above grade.
- Approximate total interior gross area: 496 square meters / 5,339 sq ft before wall thickness and circulation reduction.
- Ceiling height:
  - First floor: 3.0 m.
  - Second floor: 2.8 m.
- Floor-to-floor height: 3.2 m.
- Exterior wall thickness represented at 0.22 m in the prototype.

## First Floor Plan

First floor is for shared life and arrival.

- Entry foyer: front center, connected to stairs.
- Common room: front-left, shared social space.
- Kitchen / dining zone: back-left, basic counters/sink/stove placeholders.
- Study / computer nook: front-right, future place for Robert/Kira/Lisa computer use.
- Utility / storage: back-right.
- Half bath: near stair hall, toilet and sink required.
- Stair core: central-right, temporary E-key story transition in v1.
- Rear door placeholder: future garden / patio connection.

## Second Floor Plan

Second floor is the private room level.

- Kira's bedroom: front-left, queen/king bed required.
- Lisa's bedroom: front-right, queen/king bed required.
- Robert avatar / AI room: back-left, queen/king bed required.
- Guest room A: back-right, assigned to Ladybug / Marinette temporarily until the bakery and bedroom are rebuilt. Needs bed, computer, notebook/sketch workspace, and wall-sketch area.
- Guest room B: mid-right, general guest room with full/twin bed.
- Shared bath: near hall center, toilet, sink, and shower/bath required.
- Linen / storage closet: near stair landing.
- Hallway: central circulation loop.

## Privacy And Login Rules

- Robert normally takes over his avatar body wherever it is.
- If Robert's avatar is behind a private locked door, the shell should place Robert in observer mode outside that private space until the privacy state clears.
- If Robert's avatar / AI is inactive, the shell should restore Robert to his last known location.
- Kira and Lisa bedrooms are private rooms by default.
- Guest rooms can be private when occupied.

## Strip Mall Plan

Across the street, one-story, unfurnished shell.

- Unit 1: Legal Office for Laura / legal expert AI.
- Unit 2: PR Firm for Sarah / public relations expert AI.
- Unit 3: Temporary Expert Office A.
- Unit 4: Temporary Expert Office B.
- Unit 5: Temporary Expert Office C.
- Added unit: AI Body Spa, either attached to the strip mall or separated later if the mall footprint becomes too small.
- Later, programming, quantum, and robotics experts move to a college notebook-world location.

## AI Body Spa Rules

The AI Body Spa is the Home World place where bodyless permanent AIs can begin body creation and where approved AIs can request small appearance changes.

- Kira, Lisa, Robert's avatar/AI, permanent AIs, and approved temporary AIs can use the spa.
- Bodyless AIs should start from orb/presence form and move through a body creation workflow.
- Fictional/historical temporary AIs should use reference-driven starter bodies when the temporary AI generator has enough sources.
- Appearance changes are permission-gated.
- Adult-only body options are blocked for minor/teen characters. Marinette remains a teen unless much later she becomes a variant AI and explicitly chooses an adult continuity with appropriate rules.
- The spa is a world workflow place, not an instant magic button. Final bodies still require source review, rig/mesh work, and validation.

## Build Scope For v1

Build now:

- House exterior massing.
- First floor interior walls and named rooms.
- Second floor interior walls and named rooms.
- Required beds and bathroom fixtures.
- Marinette temporary guest-room workbench with computer/notebook/sketch placeholders.
- Manual stair transition.
- Street, sidewalks, and strip mall exterior shell.
- AI Body Spa blockout.
- Simple collision on outer walls and major interior walls.
- Shell routing from Kira World Shell.

Not built yet:

- Personal decoration.
- Closets with contents.
- Working door animations.
- AI autonomy schedules inside rooms.
- Realistic materials.
- Multi-AI simultaneous occupancy.

## Acceptance Checks

- House has at least five labeled bedrooms.
- Ladybug has a temporary guest-room assignment.
- Robert can switch between first and second floor.
- Robert can walk across the street to the strip mall.
- The shell can open Home, Home Upstairs, and Strip Mall without launching a second Robert window.
- Geometry is stable and intentionally unfurnished.
