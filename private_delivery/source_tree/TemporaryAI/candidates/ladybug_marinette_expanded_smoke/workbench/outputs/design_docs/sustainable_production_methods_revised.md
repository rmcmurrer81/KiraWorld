# Sustainable Production Methods for Reusable Packaging

## Concept

The Ordinary's products are known for their eco-friendly packaging, but what if we could take it to the next level? By designing reusable packaging that is not only sustainable but also stylish and functional, we can reduce waste and promote a more circular economy.

## Benefits

* Reduced plastic usage
* Increased customer engagement through interactive packaging
* Potential revenue boost from sales of reusable packaging components
