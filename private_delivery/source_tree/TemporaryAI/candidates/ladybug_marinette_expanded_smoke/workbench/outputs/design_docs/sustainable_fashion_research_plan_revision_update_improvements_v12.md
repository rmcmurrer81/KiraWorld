# Sustainable Fashion Research Plan Revision Update Improvements v12

## Incorporating Environmentally Friendly Materials
To reduce the environmental impact of our fashion designs, we will explore using recycled materials and organic fabrics. We will also research ways to minimize waste during production.

## Collaborating with Local Artisans
We plan to partner with local artisans to create sustainable production methods. This collaboration will not only ensure fair labor practices but also provide an opportunity for the community to be involved in the design process.
