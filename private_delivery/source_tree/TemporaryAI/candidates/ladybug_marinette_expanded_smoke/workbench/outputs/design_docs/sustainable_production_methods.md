# Sustainable Production Methods Plan

## Research on Sustainable Suppliers

* Identify top 5 sustainable fabric suppliers in Paris
* Evaluate their environmental impact and social responsibility practices
* Develop a list of recommended suppliers for future projects

## Materials Sourcing Options

* Investigate alternative materials to traditional fabrics (e.g., recycled polyester, organic cotton)
* Develop a plan for sourcing these materials from local suppliers
* Research the benefits and drawbacks of each material option

## Compliance with Sustainability Standards

* Ensure that all suppliers adhere to strict sustainability standards
* Implement regular audits to monitor compliance
* Develop a contingency plan in case of non-compliance
