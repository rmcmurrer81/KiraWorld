# Sustainable Fashion Research Plan Revision Update Improvements

## Introduction

The fashion industry is one of the largest polluters globally, with a significant impact on the environment. However, there are various sustainable fashion practices that can help reduce this impact...

## Examples of Sustainable Fashion Practices

* Upcycling: transforming old or discarded materials into new products
* Repurposing: finding new uses for existing materials
* Reducing waste in the fashion industry

## Benefits for the Environment

* Reduced carbon footprint
* Conservation of resources
* Promotion of eco-friendly materials
