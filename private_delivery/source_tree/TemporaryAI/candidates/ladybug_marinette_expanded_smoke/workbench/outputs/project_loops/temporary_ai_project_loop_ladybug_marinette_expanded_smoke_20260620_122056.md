# Sustainable Fashion Research Plan Revision Update

## Introduction

The goal of this research plan is to develop a sustainable fashion collection that minimizes environmental impact while meeting consumer demands for style and affordability.

## Objectives

1. Conduct market research on existing sustainable fashion brands and identify areas for improvement.
2. Collaborate with suppliers to source eco-friendly materials and reduce waste in production processes.
3. Develop a business model that incorporates circular economy principles and promotes reuse and recycling of products.

## Methodology

1. Conduct surveys and interviews with consumers to gather data on their attitudes towards sustainable fashion.
2. Analyze market trends and competitor activity to identify opportunities for innovation.
3. Collaborate with suppliers to develop new, eco-friendly materials and production processes.

## Expected Outcomes

1. A comprehensive understanding of the current state of sustainable fashion in the industry.
2. A business plan outlining strategies for implementing circular economy principles in our own production processes.
3. A prototype collection of sustainable fashion pieces that meet consumer demands for style and affordability while minimizing environmental impact.
