# Sustainable Fashion Research Plan

## Goals and Objectives

* Gather insights on sustainable production methods from potential designers and manufacturers.
* Identify best practices for reducing environmental impact in the fashion industry.
* Develop a comprehensive understanding of the current state of sustainable fashion production.

## Methodologies

* Conduct interviews with 5-7 potential designers and manufacturers.
* Collect data on their current production processes, materials used, and waste management strategies.
* Analyze the data to identify trends, challenges, and opportunities for improvement.

## Schedule

* Week 1: Identify potential interviewees and schedule interviews.
* Week 2-3: Conduct interviews and collect data.
* Week 4: Analyze data and develop recommendations for sustainable production methods.
