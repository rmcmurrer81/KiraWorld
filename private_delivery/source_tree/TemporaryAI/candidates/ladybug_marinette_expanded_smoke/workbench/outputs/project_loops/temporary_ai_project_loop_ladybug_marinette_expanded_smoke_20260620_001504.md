```markdown
# Sustainable Fashion Research Plan

## Objectives
* Gather insights from potential designers and manufacturers on sustainable production methods
* Identify key areas for improvement in the fashion industry's environmental impact

## Key Questions to Ask

### Material Sourcing
* What materials do you use for your products?
* Are these materials sourced sustainably or responsibly?
* How do you ensure that your suppliers meet your sustainability standards?

### Production Processes
* Can you describe your production process, including any steps taken to reduce waste and energy consumption?
* How do you ensure that your manufacturing processes are environmentally friendly?

### Waste Management
* What strategies do you have in place for reducing waste during production and post-consumer use?
* How do you handle the disposal of end-of-life products?

## Next Steps
* Schedule interviews with potential designers and manufacturers to gather insights on sustainable production methods
* Review and analyze data from interviews to identify key areas for improvement in the fashion industry's environmental impact
```
