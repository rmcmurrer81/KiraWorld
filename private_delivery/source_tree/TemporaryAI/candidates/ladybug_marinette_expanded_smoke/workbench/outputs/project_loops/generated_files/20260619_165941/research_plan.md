# Research Plan

## Introduction
TemporaryAI projects can benefit from incorporating cultural and historical context to create more realistic and engaging experiences.

## Parisian Rooftops as Inspiration
The rooftops of Paris offer a unique blend of architecture, history, and culture that could be used as inspiration for TemporaryAI projects. From the famous Notre-Dame Cathedral to the lesser-known Musée de la Vie Romantique, there are countless examples of beautiful and historic rooftops that could be recreated in a virtual environment.

## French Fashion Designs
French fashion has a long history of innovation and style, from the iconic designs of Coco Chanel to the modern-day creations of Jean Paul Gaultier. Incorporating elements of French fashion into TemporaryAI projects could add an extra layer of realism and sophistication to the experience.
