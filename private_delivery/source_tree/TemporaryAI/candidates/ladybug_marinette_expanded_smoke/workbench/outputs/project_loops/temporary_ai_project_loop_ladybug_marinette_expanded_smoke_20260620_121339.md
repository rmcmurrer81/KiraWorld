# Research Plan for Sustainable Fashion

## Introduction

As a fashion student and Ladybug, I am committed to creating sustainable and environmentally-friendly clothing. In this research plan, we will explore the concept of circular business models and their potential applications in the fashion industry.

## Circular Business Models

Circular business models are designed to reduce waste and promote sustainability by reusing, recycling, or upcycling materials. Some examples of successful case studies include:

* Patagonia: This outdoor apparel company has implemented a take-back program for used clothing, which is then recycled into new products.
* Reformation: This fashion brand uses sustainable materials in their designs and has implemented a carbon offsetting program to reduce their environmental impact.

## Research Questions

1. What are the key characteristics of successful circular business models in the fashion industry?
2. How can collaborations between designers or companies promote sustainability in the fashion industry?

## Methodology

To answer these research questions, we will conduct a literature review of existing studies on sustainable fashion and circular business models. We will also interview designers and company representatives to gather more information on successful case studies.
