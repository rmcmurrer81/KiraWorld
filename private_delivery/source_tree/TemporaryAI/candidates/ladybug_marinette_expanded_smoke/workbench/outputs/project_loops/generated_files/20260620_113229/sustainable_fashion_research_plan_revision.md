# Sustainable Fashion Research Plan Revision

## Introduction

The fashion industry is one of the largest polluters globally, with significant environmental and social impacts. This research plan aims to explore sustainable fashion practices and materials, with a focus on upcycled textiles, recycled polyester, and organic cotton.

## Upcycled Textiles

Upcycled textiles are made from post-consumer waste, reducing the need for virgin materials and minimizing waste. Examples include:

* Denim recycling programs
* Old clothing turned into new products (e.g., bags, accessories)

## Recycled Polyester

Recycled polyester is a sustainable alternative to traditional polyester, using recycled plastic bottles as raw material. Benefits include:
	+ Reduced greenhouse gas emissions
	+ Conserved water and energy resources
	+ Minimized waste

## Organic Cotton

Organic cotton is grown without synthetic pesticides or fertilizers, promoting soil health and biodiversity. Advantages include:

	+ Improved crop yields and reduced pesticide use
	+ Enhanced ecosystem services (e.g., pollination, pest control)
	+ Potential for increased water retention and reduced erosion

## Conclusion

This research plan provides a foundation for exploring sustainable fashion practices and materials. Future studies should investigate the feasibility of implementing these strategies in the fashion industry.
