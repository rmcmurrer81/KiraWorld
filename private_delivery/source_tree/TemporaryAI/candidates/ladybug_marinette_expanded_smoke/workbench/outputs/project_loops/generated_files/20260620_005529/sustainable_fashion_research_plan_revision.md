# Sustainable Fashion Research Plan Revision

## Introduction

As a fashion designer, I am committed to creating sustainable and environmentally friendly clothing. This research plan aims to explore the use of eco-friendly materials and production methods in my Parisian fashion concept.

## Eco-Friendly Materials

Inspired by The Ordinary's approach to skincare, I propose using plant-based materials such as bamboo or sugarcane for the fabric of my collection. These materials are biodegradable and require less water and energy to produce than traditional fabrics.

## Production Methods

I plan to investigate sustainable production methods such as 3D printing or digital knitting, which can reduce waste and minimize environmental impact. I will also research local and responsible suppliers who share my commitment to sustainability.

## Interview Questions

* What are the most significant challenges facing sustainable fashion designers today?
* How can we incorporate eco-friendly materials into our designs without compromising on style or quality?
* What role do you think sustainable fashion can play in reducing waste and minimizing environmental impact?
