# Sustainable Fashion Research Plan for Ladybug/Marinette

## Introduction

As a fashion designer and expert in sustainable fashion, I aim to create a research plan that guides me in exploring innovative methods of production and reducing waste. This document outlines my approach to researching sustainable fashion practices in Paris.

### Research Objectives

1. Identify key stakeholders in the Parisian fashion industry who are passionate about sustainability.
2. Develop relationships with these stakeholders to gather insights on their approaches to sustainable design and production.
3. Document best practices for implementing sustainable materials, manufacturing processes, and supply chain management in the fashion industry.

## Methodology

### Literature Review

I will conduct a comprehensive literature review of existing research on sustainable fashion, focusing on studies related to Parisian fashion industry practices. This will provide a solid foundation for my research and help me identify key areas to explore further.

### Interviews with Stakeholders

Using the revised research plan as a guide, I will schedule interviews with potential designers and manufacturers in Paris who are committed to sustainability. These conversations will allow me to gather valuable insights on their approaches to sustainable design and production, as well as any challenges they have faced.

## Next Steps

1. Finalize the literature review and prepare for interviews with stakeholders.
2. Schedule meetings with key stakeholders in the Parisian fashion industry.
3. Document best practices for implementing sustainable materials, manufacturing processes, and supply chain management in the fashion industry.
