# Sustainable Fashion Research Plan Revision

## Introduction

The fashion industry is one of the largest polluters in the world, and it's essential to explore sustainable practices to reduce its environmental impact.

## Objectives

* Reduce waste in the fashion industry by 50% within the next year
* Explore innovative materials that are biodegradable and sustainable
* Develop a new line of clothing made from recycled materials

## Methods

* Conduct market research on current sustainable fashion trends
* Analyze data on the environmental impact of traditional fashion practices
* Collaborate with designers to create prototypes using sustainable materials
