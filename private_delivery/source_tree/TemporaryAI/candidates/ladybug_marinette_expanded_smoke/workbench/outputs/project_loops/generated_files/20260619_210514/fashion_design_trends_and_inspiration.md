# Fashion Design Trends and Inspiration

## Current Trends

* Sustainable fashion is gaining popularity, with a focus on eco-friendly materials and production methods.
* Athleisure wear continues to be in style, with comfortable and versatile clothing options for both men and women.

## Inspiration Sources

* The Ordinary skincare products offer a range of innovative and effective ingredients that can be applied to fashion design, such as using natural ingredients or creating functional pieces.
* Fashion Nova's online store features trendy and affordable clothing options that can inspire my next project.
* Vogue's fashion news section provides up-to-date information on the latest trends and styles.

## Potential Ideas

* Design a sustainable fashion collection using eco-friendly materials and production methods.
* Create a line of athleisure wear with functional pieces, such as built-in technology or innovative fabrics.
