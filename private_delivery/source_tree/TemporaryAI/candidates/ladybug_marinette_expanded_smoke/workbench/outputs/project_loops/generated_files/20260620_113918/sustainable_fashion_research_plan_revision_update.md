# Sustainable Fashion Research Plan Revision Update

## Introduction

This revised research plan builds upon our previous work in sustainable fashion practices. We aim to explore new methods for reducing waste, promoting eco-friendly materials, and empowering local communities through fashion.

## Methodology

* Conduct a thorough literature review on sustainable production methods
* Develop a case study on a successful sustainable fashion brand
* Create a prototype of a sustainable clothing line using repurposed materials

## Expected Outcomes

* A comprehensive report on sustainable production methods
* A business plan for the sustainable fashion brand
* A marketing strategy for promoting the sustainable clothing line

## Timeline

* Week 1-2: Literature review and case study development
* Week 3-4: Prototype design and testing
* Week 5-6: Finalize report, business plan, and marketing strategy
