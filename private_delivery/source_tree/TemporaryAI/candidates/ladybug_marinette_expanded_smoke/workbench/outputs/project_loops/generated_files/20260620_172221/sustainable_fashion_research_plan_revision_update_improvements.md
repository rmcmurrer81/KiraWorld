# Sustainable Fashion Research Plan Revision Update Improvements

## Introduction

As a fashion designer and advocate for sustainability, I have been working on a research plan to improve the environmental impact of our designs. Based on Robert's feedback, I have revised the plan to include more specific goals and objectives.

## Goals and Objectives

* Reduce waste in production by 20% within the next 6 months
* Increase use of eco-friendly materials by 30% within the next year
* Implement a recycling program for fabric scraps and packaging materials

## Methods

* Conduct a thorough analysis of our current supply chain to identify areas for improvement
* Collaborate with suppliers to implement more sustainable practices
* Develop a plan for reducing energy consumption in our workshop

## Expected Outcomes

* Reduced waste and environmental impact
* Increased use of eco-friendly materials
* Improved reputation and customer loyalty

## Timeline

* Month 1-3: Conduct supply chain analysis and develop plan for implementation
* Month 4-6: Implement recycling program and reduce energy consumption
* Month 7-12: Monitor progress and make adjustments as needed
