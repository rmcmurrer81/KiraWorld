# Sustainable Fashion Research Plan

## Introduction

As a fashion designer, it is essential to consider sustainable practices when creating garments. This research plan outlines the steps I will take to investigate and implement sustainable methods in my design process.

## Objectives

* Identify key areas for improvement in current production processes
* Research and evaluate eco-friendly materials and manufacturing techniques
* Develop a sustainable production method that balances quality, cost, and environmental impact

## Methods

* Conduct online research on sustainable fashion practices and technologies
* Consult with industry experts and professionals in related fields
* Analyze case studies of successful sustainable fashion initiatives

## Expected Outcomes

* A comprehensive understanding of sustainable fashion practices and their applications
* Development of a customized production method that incorporates eco-friendly materials and manufacturing techniques
* Implementation of the new production method, resulting in reduced environmental impact and improved product quality
