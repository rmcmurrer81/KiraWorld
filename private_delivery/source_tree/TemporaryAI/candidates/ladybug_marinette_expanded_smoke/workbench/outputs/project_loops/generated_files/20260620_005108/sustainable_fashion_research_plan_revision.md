# Sustainable Fashion Research Plan Revision
## Material Sourcing
* Investigate local suppliers for organic cotton and recycled materials
* Consider partnering with suppliers to reduce transportation costs and carbon footprint

## Supply Chain Management
* Develop a system for tracking inventory and waste reduction
* Implement a recycling program for fabric scraps and packaging materials

## Collaborations
* Research potential partnerships with other designers or organizations to share knowledge and resources
* Explore opportunities for joint events or workshops to promote sustainable fashion practices
